What this means

You are holding revenue up with price, not demand. That works while customers accept the increases — but units, discounts and returns are all moving the wrong way at the same time, which is the pattern that usually appears one or two periods before price resistance shows up in revenue.

4Growth drivers

Revenue rose 113,000 EGP. Splitting that change shows where it came from: higher prices added 164,000 EGP, while selling fewer units gave back 51,000 EGP.

Read this with section 5

A 164,000 EGP price gain that yields only 15,400 EGP of extra gross profit means most of the price increase went to covering cost, not to margin. The growth is real; it is just not profitable growth.

Discounting is rising alongside prices

List prices rose 6.9% and discounts rose 18.4% in the same period. That combination usually means the price increase is being negotiated away at the counter — the increase is on the shelf but not in the till. Returns rising 9.2% points the same way.

The branches rank identically by revenue and by margin — Nasr City is both the biggest and the most profitable, Dokki both the smallest and the least, with no exceptions in between. Your two shrinking branches are also your two thinnest. Whatever Nasr City and Maadi are doing is worth understanding before Dokki gets more support.

8Concentration

Sales are heavily concentrated. The top three branches of six produce 71% of revenue — if sales were spread evenly, three of six would produce 50%.

Your sales Even spread (reference)
Concentration risk

Losing Nasr City would remove 29.5% of revenue and a disproportionate share of profit, since it also carries the highest margin. Concentration is not automatically bad — it often means one location is genuinely excellent — but it does mean the business has one point of failure rather than six.

9What this review does not cover

Two analyses could not be produced from the file you supplied. Everything above is unaffected by these.

Your file has no receipt or invoice number, so there is no way to tell which rows belong to the same sale. Counting rows instead would overstate basket size wherever one sale spans several lines.

The rest of this review is unaffected.

Your file covers seven months, so the same period one year earlier is not inside it. The comparison in section 3 is against the seven months immediately before.

The rest of this review is unaffected.
