Analysis results

Two families only: a transitional serif for titles, section headings and every headline figure; a humanist sans for all interface text, including Arabic, which uses the same sans across both scripts. The letterspaced mono is a third, narrow role — eyebrows, mottos and the wordmark — never body copy. Serif is never used below 16 px and never for a table cell.

Space, radius, border, shadow, icons

Spacing scale

4 px base. 4 · 8 · 12 · 14 · 18 · 20 · 24 · 26 · 32 · 44.

Card padding is 18–20 px on compact rows, 24–26 px on content cards. Section gap 24 px.

Radius

7 10 14 pill

7 file chips · 10 buttons, inputs, nav items · 14 cards and panels · pill for search, badges, tags. Nothing exceeds 18 px.

Border & shadow

Hairlines carry the structure: 1 px #ECE7DF around cards, #F2EDE5 between rows. Shadows are near-absent — a card rests on 0 1px 2px rgba(22,33,43,.04). Only floating layers (dropdown, side panel, modal) get 0 18px 40px -24px rgba(22,33,43,.28). No elevation on hover.

Icons

Line icons, 1.6–1.8 px stroke, 18 px inline / 22 px in a tile. Tiles are 46 px, radius 11, tinted to the semantic family. Never filled glyphs, never two-tone, never emoji.

Side panel & report viewer

Side panel — 360–400 px, docked at the inline end inside the content area (not overlaying the shell), white, 14 px radius, 1 px border, own header with title, severity badge and a close ×, then tabs, a definition list, related chart and one gold primary action pinned at the foot.

The rendered page is a paper sheet on the canvas tint with a 1 px border and the lightest shadow in the system.

04

Layout rules

Grid

12 columns, 20 px gutter, inside a 1440 px max. The recurring blocks are 4-up (KPI row), 3-up (equal content cards), and 2-up asymmetric at 1.6 : 1 (primary work area beside a supporting rail). Never more than four cards across.

Density

Comfortable, not compact. A dashboard screen carries at most five stacked bands: hero, KPI row, two analytic rows, footer strip. Cards in one row share a height; a card never sets its own width outside the grid.

Proportion

KPI card ≈ 3.3 : 1. Chart card 4 : 3 at 320–420 px tall. List card matches the tallest sibling and scrolls internally rather than stretching the row.

Alignment & whitespace

One inline-start alignment line runs from the page title through every card edge. Figures align to the baseline of their labels. Prose is capped at 72 ch. Whitespace is carried by padding inside white cards, not by gaps in the canvas.

Numbered stepper (done · current · waiting), a gold determinate bar with a percentage, an ETA, and a live activity timeline. Long work always shows partial results labelled Preliminary rather than an empty screen.

Loading (skeleton)

Grey #F1ECE4 bars at the true shape of the content, radius 6. No spinners inside cards; a spinner appears only in a button that is mid-submit.

Empty

Error · warning · success · disabled

Error Column contains mixed content. A suggested fix is always offered beside the message. Warning Needs review — the work continues, the row is flagged. Success Stated as a badge or a green check in a list, never as a banner that must be dismissed. Disabled #F2EEE7 ground, #AFB5BB ink, no border change, cursor not-allowed.
06 · 07

Responsive and RTL

Desktop ≥ 1280

Full shell. Rail expanded at 236 px. 4-up KPIs, 3-up cards, docked side panel, three-column report viewer.

Tablet 768–1279

Rail collapses to a 72 px icon rail with tooltips; the motto card is dropped, not shrunk. KPIs 2-up, content cards 2-up, the hero illustration crops from the inline end and the motto panel is hidden. Side panel becomes an overlay sheet at 420 px. Filters scroll horizontally.

Mobile < 768

Rail becomes a bottom tab bar of the five primary destinations plus an overflow. Everything stacks 1-up; the hero keeps the sun band at 160 px with the headline over it. Tables become stacked record cards, never a pinched grid. Report viewer: page canvas only, sections and comments move to sheets. Targets ≥ 44 px.

Arabic / RTL

Arabic is a first-class mirror, not a translation layer. Every rule here is written in logical properties, so the rail, inset selection bars, breadcrumbs, steppers and side panel all flip. What does not flip: chart time axes, numerals shown with Latin digits in mixed tables, file identifiers, and the ordering inside a version transition. Arabic sets in the same sans, one step more line-height (1.8), no letterspacing and no italic — emphasis comes from weight. The EN | عربي switch lives in the utility bar in both directions.
08

Egyptian-inspired visual language

The reference set is disciplined: the Egyptian idea appears as light, geometry and horizon, and only ever in three places — the sunrise hero band, the sidebar motto card, and the report cover. The product surface itself is quiet. That restraint is the rule, not an accident of the renders.

Permitted

  • The rising sun disc behind a horizon — the single hero motif, always warm, always low.
  • One clean pyramid silhouette and its reflection, rendered as soft masses or thin line-work, never both in the same frame.
  • The arc-and-disc mark of the wordmark, reused as a small ornament at ≤ 80 px.
  • Architectural line diagrams in gold on navy: concentric arcs, a colonnade rhythm, a single incised rule.
  • Register logic borrowed as layout — horizontal bands with shared baselines and one alignment line.
  • Bilingual motto blocks in letterspaced mono and Arabic, treated as typography rather than decoration.

Forbidden

  • Hieroglyph strips, cartouches, ankhs, eyes of Horus, scarab jewellery or gods as UI furniture.
  • Papyrus, sandstone or parchment textures behind content; the canvas is a flat warm off-white.
  • Gold as a fill on cards, tables, badges or charts — gold is ink, rule and one button.
  • Egyptian-styled display type, faux-carved letterforms, outlines or letterpress effects on text.
  • Camels, palms, deserts-as-wallpaper, tomb interiors, or any lapis-and-turquoise jewellery palette.
  • Ornament inside a data region: no motif may appear behind a chart, table, form or side panel.
Budget rule. At most one Egyptian element per viewport, and it must sit in the header band, the rail, or a cover — never in the working area. If a screen would carry two, the second becomes plain typography.

Motion and interaction states

The references are static, so every interactive state below is a decision rather than an observation. One easing, two durations, and no motion that repeats.

320 ms · panels, sheets, page-enter reveals

Max 8 px. Nothing slides further; nothing scales.

Reduced motion
All of the above collapse to an instant state change; the hero light renders at its final value.

Interaction states — try them

Primary Secondary Ghost Focus ring

Hover on a card row is a #FBF9F6 wash, no lift and no shadow change. Focus is always a 2 px #BE912E ring at 2 px offset, on every control including table rows and nav items. Pressed states darken 6%; they never translate.

The hero light

On page enter the sunrise band fades its glow from 40% to 100% over 900 ms and the disc rises 8 px into place. It happens once per navigation. No parallax, no scroll-linked movement, no ambient loop — an ambient sun turns a governed product into a screensaver.

Evidence affordance

Any published figure may carry the evidence marker: a 20 px gold-outlined disc bearing E, placed after the figure with an 8 px gap. It is a real button — 44 px hit area, focusable, labelled "View evidence for Total Revenue".
Activating it opens the standard side panel to an Evidence tab: the definition, the calculation version, the source columns and rows, the coverage statement, and a link to the underlying data. Never a tooltip, never a modal — evidence is a place you can stay in.
A figure with no reachable evidence must not carry the marker, and a figure the system declines to publish is stated in words in the place the number would have been — italic, ink-500, with its reason — rather than shown as a dash or hidden.

12

Gold budget and chart integrity

The gold budget

  • ≤ 5% of viewport pixels may be gold-filled. The hero band's warm light is exempt; it is illumination, not fill.
  • One gold-filled button per view. One gold selection marker active at a time per region.
  • Two gold elements may not be adjacent: a gold active tab above a gold primary button means the tab drops to weight-and-underline in ink.
  • Gold text is reserved for links, eyebrows, and numbers inside the navy rail — never for body copy on light ground.
  • In charts, gold marks the subject of the sentence the chart is making — one series, or one bar. If everything is gold, nothing is.

Severity must not rely on colour

High Medium Low

Each level gets a distinct shape as well as a hue — triangle, square, circle — and the word is always present. The same three shapes carry into chart legends and the report PDF, so severity survives greyscale printing and colour-blind reading.

Chart empty and insufficient states

Not enough data to chart this period 3 of 12 months have coverage. See coverage

A chart that cannot be drawn keeps its card, its title and its height, and states why in words with a route to the reason. It never collapses, never renders an axis with no series, and never interpolates across a gap — gaps break the line and are labelled.

Accessibility floor for the whole system. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and UI borders that encode state ≥ 3:1. Gold #BE912E on white measures 3.1:1 — acceptable for borders and ≥ 19 px text, not for body copy; gold body text uses #8C6B22. Every state that uses colour also uses a word, a shape, or a position.
