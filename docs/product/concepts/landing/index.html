Register IV · Why Khepri

Provable figures

Every important figure reconciles with the evidence behind it, reachable from the claim it supports.

Explicit uncertainty

Caveats and unavailable conclusions stay visible, in the place the answer would have appeared.

Bilingual parity

Arabic and English receive equivalent facts, caveats and warnings. Neither is a translation of the other.

Reproducible analysis

The same admissible inputs produce the same governed result, under a stated calculation version.

Register V · How it works

  1. I Send an export Upload the operational file you already have. No integration, no schema to match first.
  2. II Khepri establishes meaning Columns, population and coverage are resolved and shown back to you before anything is calculated.
  3. III Facts reconcile against evidence Retail calculations run deterministically. Figures that do not reconcile are not published.
  4. IV Review a decision-ready result A bilingual report carrying its figures, its caveats and its refusals together.
