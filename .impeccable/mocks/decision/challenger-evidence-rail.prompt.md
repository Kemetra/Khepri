Use case: UI mockup / marketing landing page concept.
Asset type: full-fidelity desktop first viewport, 16:9 landscape.
Create a unique KHEPRI landing-page visual direction named “The Excavated Ledger.”

Core concept: a modern retail evidence system revealed like an archaeological excavation of an ancient Egyptian administrative record. The page should unmistakably evoke ancient Egypt through authentic material and spatial cues—layered papyrus, dark basalt, limestone dust, Nile-blue faience, oxidized copper, precise incised geometry, and a restrained scarab-shaped provenance seal—while still feeling like a premium contemporary enterprise software product. This is not a museum poster or fantasy scene.

Composition: edge-to-edge dark basalt background with a monumental asymmetric grid. Top navigation is minimal: KHEPRI wordmark on the left, EN | AR language control on the right, no call-to-action. Large editorial headline at upper left: “Retail decisions you can prove.” A subtle Arabic counterpart appears with equal visual dignity. At right and below, an “excavated” papyrus evidence slab sits partly inset into the basalt, organized as horizontal strata. Each stratum reveals one admitted fact. Use carved datum lines, small registration marks inspired by ancient surveying grids, and one scarab seal only as a tiny provenance/status device. A withheld stratum is visibly interrupted and dusted out, making refusal feel intentional and trustworthy.

Show these exact governed illustrative facts and no others:
- q3-sales-export.csv
- 18 stores admitted
- Revenue — 4,182,600 EGP — PROVEN
- Transactions — 30,600 — PROVEN
- Prior-quarter comparison — WITHHELD
- Not supported by q3-sales-export.csv
- Illustrative data — not customer data

Style: refined art direction, restrained archaeology, modern editorial typography, crisp product information, highly legible, sophisticated bilingual rhythm, subtle depth and tactile lighting, no glossy 3D gimmicks.
Palette: basalt black, warm limestone, aged papyrus, Nile/lapis blue, oxidized copper, one restrained red-ochre refusal accent.
Ancient Egyptian feeling must come from material culture, axial order, record-keeping, and provenance—not from clichés.

Hard constraints: no CTA button, no signup, no request access, no testimonials, no customer logos, no pricing, no benchmarks, no AI claims, no invented integrations, no charts with unsupported trends, no external brand marks.
Avoid: pyramids as decorative background, pharaoh masks, gods, tomb-tourism imagery, excessive gold, costume hieroglyph fonts, cyberpunk, generic SaaS dashboard, generic gradient hero, glassmorphism, stock people, gaming UI.
The result should look presentation-ready and convincingly implementable as a server-rendered bilingual web page.
