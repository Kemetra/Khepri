# RRA-002: Governed File Intake and Deletion Lifecycle

## Outcome

An isolated beta session can accept one bounded retail CSV or XLSX input, store it securely, and
delete all content on demand or at expiry with content-free evidence.

## Requirements

- Enforce a 50 MB maximum before durable storage and while streaming the upload.
- Determine the supported type from content; do not trust a filename or declared media type.
- Accept CSV and a single populated XLSX worksheet. Reject empty, malformed, encrypted,
  macro-enabled, unsupported, or ambiguous multi-sheet inputs.
- Validate archive expansion and parser limits to prevent decompression and resource-exhaustion
  attacks.
- Encrypt stored input and derived content in transit and at rest in isolated object namespaces.
- Bind object metadata to its opaque owner, session, digest, size, media type, creation time, and
  expiry time without logging customer content.
- Support immediate idempotent deletion and automatic seven-day expiry across input, temporary
  materializations, facts, narrative, exports, and orphaned objects.
- Retain only content-free deletion evidence: opaque identifiers, timestamps, digests, attempted
  locations, outcomes, and retry state.

## Exclusions

External data connectors, recurring refresh, long-term archives, customer data export, and
retention beyond the beta session are not authorized.

## Verification

Tests cover content sniffing, size boundaries, malformed and empty inputs, multi-sheet handling,
encryption metadata, immediate deletion, expiry, retry safety, and orphan cleanup.

This specification is draft and does not authorize product implementation.
