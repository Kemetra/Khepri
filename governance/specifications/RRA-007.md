# RRA-007: Reliable Report Operations and Performance Evidence

## Outcome

The beta report workflow is recoverable, observable without customer content, and demonstrably
meets its approved completion-time objective.

## Requirements

- Run bounded, idempotent background jobs with explicit state transitions, leases, retry limits,
  restart recovery, and orphan detection.
- Instrument upload validation, materialization, profiling, mapping, KPI calculation, narrative
  generation, chart rendering, PDF generation, Excel generation, storage, and final delivery.
- Record queue time, processing time, retries, provider latency, dataset size, and output size
  without logging source content, safe labels, narrative, or other customer data.
- Correlate operational evidence with opaque session, job, fact-package, and report-bundle IDs.
- Make deletion jobs idempotent and retain content-free evidence of each location, attempt,
  outcome, and completion time.
- Demonstrate that at least 95% of valid beta datasets of 50 MB or less produce the complete
  bundle within ten minutes under an approved benchmark workload and environment.
- Fail CI when an approved performance tolerance is exceeded.
- Require every optimization to target a measured bottleneck, preserve byte-equivalent facts and
  equivalent citations/content, include before/after evidence, and retain validation and analysis
  depth.
- Prefer bounded parallelism, immutable-computation caching, streaming intake, incremental
  rendering, preloaded templates, and provider timeout/refusal controls when evidence supports
  them.

## Operational gate

Beta exit requires all 42 reference assessments reviewed, zero critical privacy/isolation/
deletion/numerical-integrity failures, full figure reconciliation, passing recovery and
performance evidence, and a separate beta-authorization artifact naming the client count and
observation period.

## Exclusions

Scheduling, customer notifications, multi-region operations, unbounded retries, content logging,
or latency improvements that weaken controls are not authorized.

## Verification

Tests cover idempotency, restarts, provider failure/refusal, partial export failure, deletion
retries, orphan cleanup, per-stage telemetry, content-free logging, benchmark identity, and
performance-regression enforcement.

This specification is draft and does not authorize product implementation.
