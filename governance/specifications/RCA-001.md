# RCA-001: Commercial Identity, Organizations, and Authorization

## Outcome

A person holds a durable account, belongs to one or more organizations, acts in exactly one of
them at a time, and reaches the retail reporting capabilities `RRA-001` through `RRA-009` already
provide — while every isolation, privacy, and deletion control those specifications fix continues
to hold, unweakened and independently testable.

## Requirements

### Accounts

- **FR-001**: Creating an account MUST establish a durable identity that survives session end,
  and MUST NOT itself grant access to any organization or to any retail content.
- **FR-002**: An account credential MUST be stored only as a strong salted hash, never in a form
  from which the credential can be recovered.
- **FR-003**: Authentication MUST establish exactly one authenticated actor for the resulting
  session, and MUST record no retail content in that session's identity record.
- **FR-004**: A failed authentication MUST NOT reveal whether the account exists, whether it is
  disabled, or which check failed. All failure paths MUST be indistinguishable to the caller.
- **FR-005**: Account recovery MUST issue a single-use, expiring recovery secret, stored only as a
  strong salted hash, and MUST invalidate that secret on first successful use.
- **FR-006**: Initiating recovery for an address that has no account MUST be externally
  indistinguishable from initiating recovery for one that does.
- **FR-007**: Completing recovery MUST invalidate every pre-existing authentication session for
  that account.
- **FR-008**: A disabled account MUST fail authentication, and MUST have every pre-existing
  session cease to authorize any action, with no dependence on session expiry to take effect.

### Organizations and membership

- **FR-009**: An organization MUST be a durable scope that owns retail content, distinct from the
  accounts that may act in it.
- **FR-010**: Creating an organization MUST establish its creator as an owner-role member in the
  same operation, so that no organization exists without an owner at any observable instant.
- **FR-011**: An account MUST be able to hold membership in more than one organization
  simultaneously, and those memberships MUST NOT merge, share, or otherwise widen each other's
  data scope.
- **FR-012**: Revoking one membership MUST leave every other membership of the same account, and
  every other member of the same organization, unaffected.
- **FR-013**: An organization MUST NOT reach a state with zero owner-role members. An operation
  that would remove, downgrade, or disable the final owner MUST fail closed and MUST state that
  the final owner cannot be removed.
- **FR-014**: Every change to a membership or a role MUST record which authenticated account made
  it, which membership it affected, what the prior and resulting roles were, and when it occurred.
- **FR-015**: The role model for this specification MUST consist of exactly two roles: `owner` and
  `member`. An `owner` MAY perform every `member` action, plus invite, change roles, revoke
  memberships, and disable the organization. Any further role requires a separate specification.

### Invitations

- **FR-016**: An invitation MUST name exactly one organization and one intended role, MUST carry a
  high-entropy secret persisted only as a strong salted hash, and MUST carry an explicit expiry.
- **FR-017**: An invitation MUST be redeemable at most once. A replayed, expired, revoked, or
  malformed invitation MUST fail closed without revealing which check failed.
- **FR-018**: Accepting a valid invitation MUST create exactly one membership in the named
  organization at the named role.
- **FR-019**: An invitation MUST be issuable to a person who has no account yet, and accepting it
  MUST require that an authenticated account exists at the moment of acceptance.
- **FR-020**: Revoking a membership MUST invalidate that member's unredeemed invitations to that
  organization.

### Authorization

- **FR-021**: Every protected action MUST resolve exactly one authenticated actor and, where the
  action touches organization-owned content, exactly one explicit organization scope.
- **FR-022**: Authorization MUST deny by default. An action reached without a resolved actor,
  without a resolved organization scope where one is required, or without a rule that explicitly
  permits it, MUST be denied.
- **FR-023**: Possession of an object identifier MUST confer no authority. Authorization MUST be
  decided from the actor's membership in the scope that owns the object, never from the caller's
  ability to name the object.
- **FR-024**: A request whose actor and whose named organization scope disagree — including a
  scope the actor has no membership in — MUST fail closed.
- **FR-025**: A denial for an object the actor may not reach MUST be indistinguishable from a
  denial for an object that does not exist. Denials MUST NOT disclose existence, ownership, or
  the identity of another organization.
- **FR-026**: Authorization MUST be decided at one canonical checkpoint that every protected
  action passes through. An action that does not pass through it MUST be unreachable rather than
  permitted.

### Active organization context

- **FR-027**: An authenticated session MUST carry at most one active organization at a time.
- **FR-028**: An authenticated account with no membership in any organization MUST be able to
  authenticate and MUST be denied every organization-scoped action until a membership exists.
- **FR-029**: Switching the active organization MUST succeed only into an organization in which
  the actor holds a current membership, and MUST take effect for every subsequent authorization
  decision in that session.
- **FR-030**: A change to membership or role MUST take effect for authorization decisions made
  after it, without requiring the affected session to end. A session whose active organization
  membership has been revoked MUST cease to authorize actions in that organization.

### Bridge to the existing isolation boundary

- **FR-031**: A resolved authenticated actor plus a resolved active organization MUST map to the
  governed opaque isolation scope that `RRA-001` established, and every `RRA` capability MUST
  continue to be reached only through that scope.
- **FR-032**: The commercial identifiers `email address`, `organization name`, `organization
  slug`, `customer-visible account identifier`, and `human-readable resource identifier` MUST NOT
  appear in, be derivable from, or be used as any component of an analytical isolation key.
- **FR-033**: The opaque isolation scope MUST remain opaque: it MUST contain no email address, no
  organization name, no profile identifier, and no business identity, preserving `RRA-001`'s
  identifier-opacity requirement unchanged.
- **FR-034**: Cross-scope access MUST continue to fail closed with a uniform, content-free
  refusal, preserving the existing behaviour in which a refusal discloses nothing about the
  resource.
- **FR-035**: Two distinct organizations MUST resolve to two distinct isolation scopes, and one
  organization MUST resolve to a stable scope across sessions, across active-organization
  switches, and across membership changes that do not delete the organization.
- **FR-036**: `RCA` MUST perform no authoritative retail calculation. Every retail fact, narrative,
  and report a commercial actor sees MUST originate from the existing `RRA` fact package and
  report contracts.

### Preservation of RRA controls

- **FR-037**: This specification MUST NOT weaken `RRA-001`'s or `RRA-002`'s controls: opaque
  identifiers, cross-session isolation failing closed, encryption in transit and at rest, isolated
  object namespaces, immediate idempotent deletion, and content-free logging. Each MUST remain
  covered by its existing tests, unmodified.
- **FR-038**: Consent before upload, the immutable automated-report disclosure, the reconciliation
  and provenance controls, and Arabic/English language parity MUST continue to hold for a
  commercial actor exactly as they hold for a beta participant.
- **FR-039**: `RRA` MUST remain independently testable: its existing tests MUST pass without an
  account, an organization, or a membership existing.
- **FR-040**: Identity, membership, and authorization records MUST be logged content-free. No
  credential, recovery secret, invitation secret, or retail content may appear in any log.

| 3 | Failed authentication | Refusal is uniform; existence is not disclosed | FR-004 |

## Exclusions

This specification does not authorize, and no implementation under it may introduce:

- Persistent customer workspaces, report history, retention changes, or multi-dataset storage or
  analysis. Durable retention of customer retail content requires its own Constitution VII
  decision and is not granted here.
- Billing, pricing, plans, entitlements, subscriptions, quotas, payments, or invoicing.
- Agency portfolios, client switching, delegated access, or white labeling.
- Recurring or scheduled report delivery.
- Public self-serve signup and the pre-purchase product surface.
- Any `Seshat-BI` integration, AI-generated insight, database connector, forecasting,
  customer-authored formula, or generic non-retail analysis.
- Organization deletion, and deletion or export of an account. `RRA-002`'s immediate idempotent
  content deletion is preserved unchanged by `FR-037` but is not extended to commercial identity.
- Runtime, provider, deployment, or provisioning selection, which remains governed by a separate
  architecture decision and is a distinct gate from anything this specification requires.
- Any role beyond `owner` and `member`, and any broad refactoring of `RRA`.
- Product implementation while this specification remains `draft`.

## Assumptions

Each assumption is resolved from repository evidence and is stated so a reviewer can reject it.

- **A-1**: An account is identified to a person by an email address, and that address is unique
  across accounts. Uniqueness is treated as a **product rule** here — it is what makes "recover my
  account" and "invite this person" well defined — not merely an implementation convenience.
- **A-2**: Storing an email address is new data use under Constitution VII. `KHEPRI-DEC-014` §2
  makes collecting an email owner key permitted to specify while requiring "its own retention
  decision" before it is implemented. This specification therefore names that decision as an
  implementation precondition and does not author it.
- **A-3**: The `RRA` opaque owner identifier survives as the internal boundary key, per
  `KHEPRI-DEC-014` §2 and `RRA-001`'s requirement to "preserve the opaque owner ID as the only
  future attachment point for separately approved commercial authentication." An organization,
  not an account, is what maps to it, because organizations own content and accounts do not.
- **A-4**: Authentication is by a credential the account holder supplies. No external identity
  provider is assumed, and none is excluded; that is a plan-level decision constrained by
  `FR-002`, not a product requirement.

## Verification

- Tests cover all 20 scenarios above, each named for the scenario it verifies.
- An authorization matrix test asserts, for every combination of `{owner, member, non-member,
  unauthenticated}` against every protected action, that the outcome is the specified permit or
  the specified fail-closed denial.
- Cross-organization isolation tests assert that a member of one organization can neither read nor
  mutate another organization's content, and that the refusal is indistinguishable from the
  refusal for a nonexistent object.
- A bridge test asserts that two organizations resolve to two distinct opaque isolation scopes,
  that one organization's scope is stable across sessions and switches, and that no commercial
  identifier named in `FR-032` appears in a resolved scope.
- The existing `RRA` test suites pass unmodified, with no account, organization, or membership
  present, evidencing `FR-039`.
- `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and `uv run pytest` pass.

## Implementation preconditions

Implementation under this specification requires all of the following, per
`KHEPRI-DEC-014` §2a and §3. None is granted by this document.

1. This specification is `approved` in `governance/registries/specifications.yaml`.
2. A separately approved architecture decision has settled runtime and provider selection.
   `KHEPRI-DEC-008` is `proposed` and this precondition is not met.
3. A Constitution VII retention decision covering durable commercial identity data has been
   approved, per **A-2**.
4. The implementation slice links this specification and the reference assessments relevant to it.
