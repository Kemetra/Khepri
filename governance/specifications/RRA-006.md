# RRA-006: Unified Web PDF and Excel Report Bundle

## Outcome

One report bundle exposes the same fact-package version and grounded narrative through accessible
web, PDF, and Excel surfaces in Arabic and English.

## Requirements

- Define `ReportBundle` to bind every output to one immutable fact-package version, narrative
  version, source digest, mapping version, and generation timestamp.
- Render all figures, charts, labels, caveats, and citations from the fact package; reject any
  surface that cannot reconcile to it.
- Provide equivalent Arabic and English facts and caveats with correct language metadata and
  right-to-left Arabic layout.
- Generate a navigable web report, tagged and readable PDF, and structured Excel workbook with
  accessible tables, units, formats, source/citation sheets, and machine-readable provenance.
- Clearly disclose that the analysis is automatically generated and whether narrative was
  refused or omitted.
- Treat partial export failure as an incomplete bundle; retry idempotently without delivering a
  mixture of versions.
- Store published outputs under the same session expiry and deletion boundary as the input.

## Two-population bundle

A two-population bundle presents one `RRA-008` §Two-population comparison — the facts derived
under `rra008.crossversion.v1` over an ordered pair of dataset versions — through the same three
surfaces, in both languages, under the same reconciliation discipline as a report bundle. It
authorizes the surface half of the scope `G4-01` defines and `RRA-008` excludes, and nothing beyond.

It is a **sibling of `ReportBundle`, not a section within one**, for three reasons this specification
and its consumers already fix. The Requirements above bind a report bundle to *one* fact-package
version, source digest and mapping version, and a two-population bundle has two source digests.
The governed section order a report bundle may not depart from is closed, and the section named
`comparison` within it is period comparison under `rra008.comparison.v2`, which a cross-version
figure must never be recorded under. And a figure inside a report bundle reconciles to the one
package that bundle names, which a figure comparing two packages cannot do. `RRA-008` placed its
two-population *family* beside the four existing families as a new admission rather than a change
to them, and this bundle sits beside the report bundle in the same way. Which type carries the
two-population fact is `RRA-008`'s question, and this specification neither answers nor depends on
it: a surface reproduces a fact's stated values and provenance whatever carries them.

### Identity

A two-population bundle binds every output to:

- the **ordered pair** of fact-package identities, subject first and baseline second — the operand
  order `RRA-008` §Composite provenance makes part of fact identity — each carrying its source digest,
  its coverage-manifest identity **and** its coverage signature identities, for the reason the report
  bundle's identity carries both: two packages built from one upload under different coverage state
  identical figures while their evidence differs, and an identity that cannot tell them apart is not
  one; the reversed pair is a different bundle with a different identity;
- the **one** package version, formula version and mapping version both packages share. `RRA-008`
  `D-2`–`D-4` refuse a pair that differs in any of them before a figure exists, so this bundle never
  binds to two of any version and records one of each;
- the governed comparison formula version **`rra008.crossversion.v1`**, recorded and never confused
  with `rra008.comparison.v2`;
- the narrative version, and this bundle's own version identifier, **`rra006.crossversion.bundle.v1`**,
  which is new, succeeds nothing, and is not a successor to the report bundle's version.

### Surfaces and reconciliation

The three surfaces, the two languages, the right-to-left Arabic layout, the automatic-generation
disclosure, and the publish-together-or-not-at-all rule are the Requirements above, unchanged. In
addition:

- Every figure a surface shows is a `rra008.crossversion.v1` fact's subject value, baseline value,
  or a delta that fact states, as `RRA-008` §Operand order states them. A surface never shows a
  delta without both operands beside it, and never shows a percentage delta the fact refused.
- Reconciliation refuses a surface that presents any figure neither package supplied and no
  cross-version fact states, and refuses a surface whose two languages show different cells —
  the existing cross-surface and cross-language rules, applied to a bundle with two sources.
- Every surface carries `RRA-008` §Refusals and caveats' admitted-pair caveat in both languages,
  because a reader who takes a cross-version delta for a change in trading has been misled.
- Every figure's evidence record discloses both versions' identifiers, both cited retained bases,
  both coverage-manifest identities, and the operand order — the four disclosures `RRA-008`
  §Composite provenance names, carried in the same per-citation evidence record shape the report
  bundle already uses. That shape is consumed by analogy, not by dependency: the specification that
  defines it for the report bundle depends on this one, so no registry edge can point at it. This
  bundle derives its facts during assembly and retains them nowhere, and its evidence records say
  so rather than naming a retained record.

### Refusal

A pair `RRA-008` refuses produces **no bundle**: there is no figure to bind an identity to, and
`RRA-008` §Admission derives nothing for a refused pair — no partial, indicative, or best-effort
figure. What is delivered is the refusal cause's complete wording in both languages, as every
customer-facing text of this specification already must be, and nothing else. A refusal is not a
bundle with a refused section.

### Not stored

**A two-population bundle is rendered on request and retained nowhere.** This is this
specification's own rule, stated here: the Requirements above store published outputs under the
input's expiry, and a two-population bundle has no single input and no analysis run of its own to
be stored with, so nothing of it is written to any store and there is nothing to sweep, tombstone
or delete. It is consistent with, and adds nothing to, the retention decisions already in force —
`RCA-005` §Comparison retention derives a comparison at read time with no retained content class,
and `KHEPRI-DEC-033` §2 retains report bundle artifacts *with their analysis run*. The consequences
are stated rather than left to a slice:

- The three surfaces are rendered in one request and delivered in that response, or the request
  refuses. Partial rendering is an incomplete bundle exactly as the Requirements state, and retry is
  idempotent because `RRA-008` guarantees byte-equivalent reruns for the same ordered pair.
- Deleting either source version makes the bundle unavailable, not stale: a bundle derived on
  request from a version that no longer exists has nothing to derive from, and no copy exists to
  serve instead.
- **A slice that stores, lists, or reopens a two-population bundle as a report artifact is outside
  this specification** within the meaning of Constitution IV. Such a slice would need this section
  amended and a retention row for the new class in the governing retention decision first.

### Not authorized here

- **Any change to the report bundle.** Its identity, its governed section order, and its reconciliation
  stand unchanged; this bundle is beside it, not inside it.
- **Orchestration.** Who may request a comparison, the pairwise limit, and the audit event are
  `RCA-005` `FR-130`–`FR-133`'s, and the reachability `FR-132` requires is a workspace link to a
  rendering this specification governs, not a stored artifact.
- **Any calculation.** Every delta, admission predicate and refusal cause is `RRA-008`'s; a surface
  reproduces and never computes, as the Exclusions below already require.
- **Three or more populations**, deferred with `RRA-008`.

## Exclusions

White labeling, customer-authored templates, uncited exports, independent surface calculations,
long-lived report history, and a two-population bundle stored, listed, or reopened as a report
artifact are not authorized.

## Verification

Tests cover cross-surface reconciliation, Arabic/English parity, RTL rendering, accessibility,
PDF and Excel fidelity, citation links, automatic-report disclosure, deterministic regeneration,
and partial-failure recovery. For the two-population bundle: the reversed pair yielding a
distinct bundle identity; every surface showing both operands beside every delta and the
admitted-pair caveat in both languages; a refused pair delivering wording and no bundle; a
request writing no row to any table; the `rra008.crossversion.v1` version recorded on every
evidence record and never `rra008.comparison.v2`; `rra006.crossversion.bundle.v1` recorded on the
bundle's identity document, distinct from the report bundle's own version; both package identities
with their source digests, coverage-manifest identities and coverage signature identities, the one
shared package, formula and
mapping version, and the operand order recorded on the identity document, with both versions'
identifiers, both retained bases, both coverage-manifest identities and the operand order on every
evidence record; and a bundle whose source version has been deleted refusing rather than serving a
previously rendered result.
