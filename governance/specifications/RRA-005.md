# RRA-005: Grounded Arabic and English Narrative

## Outcome

A replaceable narrative provider returns Arabic and English prose grounded only in approved,
cited facts or returns an explicit refusal.

## Requirements

- Define `NarrativeAdapter` to accept only approved aggregate facts, safe labels, caveats,
  language instructions, and citation identifiers from one fact-package version.
- Never transmit raw rows, source column values, unapproved personal data, secrets, opaque owner
  identifiers, or storage locations to the provider.
- Require contractual provider controls that disable training on Khepri data and provider
  retention; final provider selection needs a separate approved architecture decision.
- Validate the response against supplied fact IDs and citations and reject unsupported numbers,
  citations, claims, or unsafe label transformations.
- Require equal factual and caveat coverage in Arabic and English; wording may differ without
  changing meaning.
- State every figure in prose in the display form the report's own tables give that figure in
  that language. That form is digit grouping and a proportion's percentage form in both
  languages, plus Arabic-Indic digits, the Arabic decimal and group separators, and the Arabic
  percent sign in Arabic. The request supplies each figure's display form in each language, as it
  already supplies a proportion's percentage form, and the provider quotes it and never converts
  a figure itself. The display form is the tables' presentation (`RRA-006` "units, formats"),
  consumed by analogy rather than by dependency, because `RRA-006` depends on this specification.
  The display form is a presentation of a supplied figure, not a new number. Grounding
  normalizes digits, separators and the percent sign before it compares, and a stated figure
  whose significant digits differ from every supplied figure stays an unsupported number. The
  figures stated in the two languages are compared for equality after the same normalization.
- Support provider timeout, failure, and refusal without inventing fallback analysis. A
  deterministic facts-only report remains available when approved by the delivery contract.
- Record provider adapter version, request fact-package version, timing, validation outcome, and
  refusal reason without logging customer content.

## Exclusions

Raw-data prompting, model-generated calculations, provider-specific business logic, hidden human
approval, and uncited prose are not authorized.

## Verification

Tests prove request minimization, absence of raw rows, number and citation grounding, bilingual
fact/caveat parity, provider replacement, timeout behavior, and safe refusal. They also prove that
every figure stated in prose is byte-identical to the table's rendering of that figure in that
language, including Arabic-Indic digits and separators in Arabic, and that a figure whose
significant digits were changed is still refused.
