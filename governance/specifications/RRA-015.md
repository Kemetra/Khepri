# RRA-015: Governed Chart Grammar and Report-Surface Presentation Evidence

## Outcome

The governed charts already rendered on the `RRA` report and evidence surfaces carry the approved
chart grammar — labelled axes with unit and period, a legend only where two or more series exist,
a visible zero baseline, stated missing data, a governed refusal in place of a drawn empty figure,
an accessible title and description, an evidence entry point, and a mirrored category axis in
Arabic — and those surfaces carry reproducible accessibility and visual-regression evidence against
the approved visual reference pack.

Every figure a chart draws is still `RRA-004`'s, `RRA-008`'s and `RRA-006`'s. This specification
adds presentation and evidence over facts that already exist, and adds no fact.

## Why this artifact exists

`docs/product/KHEPRI_UI_UX_MASTER_SPEC.md` merged at `0d3cf87` (`#475`) and the approved visual
reference pack at `e9726b5` (`#476`). Between them they settle the design direction for every
Khepri surface. Neither is implementation authority, and both say so: the master specification's
§Standing records that it "grants no implementation authority", and the pack's README records
"**Authority:** none of its own."

What the master specification could not license, it named. §18.2 lists four items as
"specification-ready but implementation-blocked", and §18.4 asks for successor authority over
`src/khepri/rra/rendering/charts.py`, `rendering/templates/_chart.svg.j2`, the shell stylesheets,
and the affected test paths. The blockers it cites are exact: `RRA-012` §Exclusions excludes "a
chart grammar, a chart type, or any charting behavior — `U1-03` names that work and it needs its
own authority", `RRA-012` §Implementation preconditions item 2 states that "`U1-03`, `U1-05`,
`U1-06`, and `U1-07` are **not** authorized here", and `RCA-008` §Exclusions repeats that the
chart-grammar, navigation, accessibility-evidence and visual-regression programmes "remain
`U1-03`, `U1-05`, `U1-06` and `U1-07`'s and need their own authority".

`charts.py` and `_chart.svg.j2` are named by **no active specification**. `RRA-006` requires that
charts render from the fact package and fixes no grammar over them; `RRA-012` excludes charting by
name. This specification is the receiving authority for that gap, in the same shape `RRA-012`
received the data-display component layer that `RRA-011` disclaimed.

**Why this is two artifacts and not the single one §18.4 asked for.** The specification template at
`governance/templates/specification.md` requires a specification to "include exactly one family in
`depends_on`", and `khepri_gov`'s `_validate_family_links` enforces it: a specification depending on
zero or two families fails validation. `RRA-012` §Outcome reads that rule as forbidding one
document across two families' surfaces — "Governing both from one document would put a single
specification across two families' surfaces, which the one-family rule in
`governance/templates/specification.md` forbids" — and, on that reading, declines the commercial
shell's stylesheets and assigns them to `RCA`.
`U1-03`'s files are `RRA` rendering paths and `U1-05`'s are the commercial shell's, so one artifact
could not carry both without contradicting an active specification on the day it merged. The work
is therefore split along the boundary the repository already draws: this specification carries
`U1-03` and the `RRA` report surfaces' share of `U1-06` and `U1-07`; active `RCA-010` carries
`U1-05`, the shell presentation surfaces, and their share of `U1-06` and `U1-07`. Neither widens
the other, and no new governance family is created.

**This specification consumes the design direction; it does not redesign it.** The chart grammar it
authorizes is the one the master specification §8 already fixes, its accessibility floors are §11's,
its visual acceptance rules are §16.3 and §17's, and its asset policy is §7's. Where this document
and the master specification appear to differ on a design rule, the master specification is the
design direction and this document is corrected; where this document and an active specification
differ on what a figure means or who may see it, the specification wins, exactly as the master
specification §A.3 already states.

## Requirements

Numbering continues from `RCA-009`, which ends at `FR-180`.

### Chart grammar

- **FR-181**: A chart renders only the governed kinds already shipped — `CHART_BAR`,
  `CHART_GROUPED_BAR`, and `CHART_LINE`. No slice under this specification admits a new chart kind,
  and no chart kind is selected by anything other than the governed section that carries it.
- **FR-182**: A chart computes no figure. It resolves the values it is given into geometry and
  applies no sum, average, difference, rank, score, normalization, threshold, percentage,
  interpolation, re-rounding, or reformatting. A chart-derived fact is a defect against this
  requirement whatever its size, and the table beside the chart remains the authoritative
  presentation.
- **FR-183**: Chart chrome states its scope. An axis is labelled with its unit and its period, a
  legend renders only where two or more series exist, and a negative value hangs below a visible
  zero baseline. **An axis truncated on a comparison is refused**, and no tooltip or hover state is
  the sole carrier of a value.
- **FR-184**: Every customer-visible word a chart renders comes from a governed code resolved in
  `wording.py`, in both languages, under the same import-time completeness assertion the tables
  around it use. A chart composes no sentence, and a chart chrome label names a part of the chart
  and never describes a metric, refusal, caveat, population, or version — where it would, `RRA-012`
  `FR-095` governs it and the word is `RRA-009`'s or `RRA-011`'s.
- **FR-185**: Missing data renders as a visible gap carrying its stated reason, and is never
  interpolated, never drawn as zero, and never silently dropped. Incomplete data renders with its
  caveat adjacent.
- **FR-186**: A refused figure replaces the chart region with the governed refusal presentation
  rather than drawing an empty chart, and a zero denominator is that refusal rather than `0%` or
  `NaN`. A refusal never shares an element or a class with an error, and never carries error paint.
- **FR-187**: Every chart carries an evidence entry point to the claim it draws, reached from the
  chart rather than from a global destination.
- **FR-188**: In Arabic the category axis runs right to left through the existing `mirrored` plot
  flag, numbers are locale-formatted with Arabic-Indic digits, Arabic month names, and the Arabic
  percent mark, and every label, axis, legend, caveat and refusal present in one language is
  present in the other. Presentation uses logical CSS properties only; a physical directional
  property introduced on a chart rule is outside this specification.

### Accessibility and visual evidence on the `RRA` report surfaces

- **FR-189**: A chart exposes `role="img"` with a `<title>` and a `<desc>`, both resolved from
  governed codes, and the surrounding surface meets the master specification §11 floors on the
  report and evidence surfaces: a visible focus on every tab stop, focus order following document
  order with no positive `tabindex`, exactly one `h1` with no skipped heading level, labels
  associated with controls, `role="status"` for refusals and progress, non-color differentiation
  for every trust state, targets of at least 44px on the element a pointer lands on, text scaling
  to 200% without loss of content or function, and `lang` and `dir` server-computed. These are
  asserted per surface as tests, in both languages.
- **FR-190**: Visual-regression evidence compares a rendered report surface against approved design
  evidence on **hierarchy, density, spacing rhythm, typography, visual states, RTL, responsive
  behaviour, and asset fidelity**. Pixel-identical equality is **not** required where responsive
  layout legitimately adapts; visual-language drift is a defect even where every other test passes.
  The evidence is deterministic and reproducible from the repository, and uses the browser
  automation the repository already ships rather than a new visual-testing platform, service, or
  hosted baseline store.
- **FR-191**: Visual evidence is evidence, not truth. No baseline, screenshot, or reference image
  is read as a source of a figure, a route, a capability, a refusal reason, or a governed word, and
  no slice copies a value out of one. Where an image and an active specification disagree, the
  specification wins.

### Assets

- **FR-192**: The master specification §7 asset policy is binding and unrelaxed. No CSS-drawn
  illustration, div-art, CSS pyramid, CSS scarab, pseudo-element used as artwork, emoji icon,
  Unicode glyph standing in for an icon, or one-off inline SVG illustration authored by an
  implementer. No external font, image, style, script, or CDN dependency, which the shipped
  `default-src 'none'` CSP forbids in any case. An icon or typeface enters only through the
  existing licence-plus-audited-digest process, which this specification does not amend and does
  not bypass. **Data-driven charts are the one expected programmatic drawing**, because they are
  drawn from governed figures; that exception reaches charts and nothing else. Where an approved
  asset is absent, the surface ships without it rather than with an approximation.

### Remediation of recorded `FR-189` failures

- **FR-193**: The five `FR-189` failures slice 9a recorded as `xfail(strict=True)` on the report and
  evidence surfaces may be corrected, and **only those five**. They are: the 44px pointer-target
  floor on the report surface's “On this page” navigation anchors, which render at 21px in both
  languages; the refusal panel's `role`; and the evidence surface's ~184px horizontal overflow at a
  390px viewport under 200% text, in both languages. A correction is admitted only where a strict
  `xfail` already records the failure, so the remediation's extent is fixed by merged evidence
  rather than by an implementer's reading. **The strict markers are the completion test**: each is
  deleted in the same slice that makes its assertion pass, and an `xfail` that starts passing while
  its marker stands is a failure of this requirement, not a success.

  **Three corrections, each bounded to a mechanism.** (1) A minimum pointer-target size on the
  existing report navigation anchors, expressed as a logical property. (2) `role="status"` in place
  of `role="note"` on the existing `refusal_panel` macro's existing element. (3) Narrow-viewport
  reflow rules for the evidence figures table, expressed as logical properties. No element, macro,
  class, component, or state is added, removed, renamed, or reordered by any of the three.

  **The `role` change is an accessibility-attribute correction, not a change of component
  semantics, and this document says so rather than leaving a reviewer to infer it.** `FR-189`
  already requires `role="status"` for refusals; the element, its `data-component` value, its
  class, and its prose — which is `RRA-009`'s and which this layer does not author — are
  unchanged. What changes is whether a screen reader announces a refusal the sighted reader can
  already see. The refusal's *meaning* is `RRA-009`'s and is untouched; its *announcement* is a
  master specification §11 floor this specification is already obliged to measure. Were this read
  instead as component semantics, the correction would belong to `RRA-012` and this requirement
  would not authorize it — so the reading is stated here, in the document that authorizes it, and
  a slice may not substitute its own.

  **What `FR-193` does not admit**, for the avoidance of a convenient reading: no new component,
  state, refusal cause, caveat, or governed word; no wording change in any language; no chart
  change; no calculation or derived value; no route, renderer, materialization, persistence, or
  asset change; no JavaScript; no telemetry; no change to `html.py`, `wording.py`, `pdf.py`,
  `excel.py`, or any composition path; no general accessibility redesign of either surface; and no
  physical directional property, `FR-186`'s logical-property discipline being unrelaxed here.

## Exclusions

This specification authorizes none of the following, and a slice claiming it does is outside its
specification within the meaning of Constitution IV:

- Any new metric, population, formula, reason code, caveat code, figure, semantic view, view
  version, or analytical calculation. It draws what `RRA-004`, `RRA-006`, `RRA-008`, `RRA-009`,
  `RRA-011` and `RRA-014` already govern.
- Any template-side delta, ratio, total, share, ranking, or other arithmetic, in a template, a
  chart module, a stylesheet, or a test fixture presented as product output.
- Any new chart kind, any forecast, trend line, projection, extrapolation, smoothing, or
  annotation not carried by a governed fact.
- Any new refusal cause, any softening, substitution, or suppression of an existing one, and any
  presentation implying an answer where the governed result is a refusal.
- The commercial shell, its stylesheets, templates, routes, assets, navigation, and filters. Those
  are `RCA`'s and `RCA-010`'s.
- The `/beta` journey surfaces, `journey.css`, the journey templates and scripts, and the pending
  `RRA-010` journey-adoption reading, which this document neither takes nor pre-empts.
- Any new route, any change to an existing route's contract, any change to the asset allowlist, and
  any new asset filename.
- Any change to the report bundle contract, the renderer protocol, the materialization path, the
  Excel renderer, the pipeline, or the renderer wiring.
- Any persistence, schema, migration, cache, pre-aggregation, materialized view, sampling, or
  retained layout, filter, sort, or dismissal state.
- Any telemetry event, product-analytics event, counter, access record, audit record, or
  content-bearing log, of any kind, content-free or not. `KHEPRI-DEC-015` §3 stands unamended.
- Any change to privacy, retention, provenance, evidence derivation, semantic admission,
  organization isolation, authorization, cross-organization boundaries, or product capability
  exposure.
- Any new package, dependency, CI workflow, or runtime behaviour.
- A general accessibility or visual-regression programme over surfaces this specification does not
  govern, and any surface-inventory guard beyond the surfaces in §Scope.

## Higher-order invariants

Presentation may change. Truth may not.

- A chart displays governed truth and does not create it. No governed calculation, metric
  definition, semantic admission rule, evidence derivation, provenance record, privacy boundary,
  retention horizon, organization isolation boundary, authorization decision, refusal semantic,
  cross-organization boundary, or exposed product capability is altered by any slice under this
  document, and the `M4` behaviour already accepted is unchanged.
- Where this document and an active specification disagree on what a figure means, who may see it,
  or what is retained, the active specification wins.
- A refusal is a governed result the reader is entitled to, never an error to be hidden or styled
  into something else.
- The approved visual reference pack is acceptance evidence for the surfaces it covers and silent
  on the rest; it carries no authority of its own and supplies no product fact.

## Verification

Every slice under this specification proves, with focused tests:

- Each governed chart kind renders its grammar: labelled axes carrying unit and period, a legend
  only with two or more series, a visible zero baseline with negative values below it, and no
  truncated axis on a comparison.
- A chart module and a chart template perform no arithmetic, by a static check over the authorized
  paths asserting no sum, difference, ratio, ranking, or percentage is derived there, and by a test
  rendering a figure whose precision differs from any default and asserting the given value
  survives unchanged.
- Missing data renders a visible gap with its stated reason rather than an interpolation or a zero;
  a refused figure renders the governed refusal rather than an empty chart; a zero denominator is a
  refusal rather than `0%` or `NaN`.
- Every chart word resolves from a governed code present in both languages, through the same
  import-time completeness assertion the surrounding tables use, and an unknown code fails closed
  rather than rendering the code string, an empty element, or a blank.
- A chart carries `role="img"` with a `<title>` and a `<desc>` from governed codes, and an evidence
  entry point reaching the claim it draws.
- Arabic renders the mirrored category axis, Arabic-Indic digits, Arabic month names and the Arabic
  percent mark, and Arabic and English carry equal labels, caveats, refusals and evidence paths.
- The `FR-189` accessibility floors hold on the report and evidence surfaces in both languages at
  the supported viewports, with no page-level horizontal overflow at any width. **Where a floor
  does not yet hold, the failure is recorded as `xfail(strict=True)` and corrected under
  `FR-193`**; the five such markers merged at `9342720`/`692134e` (`#497`/`#498`) are the whole
  of what `FR-193` admits, and the requirement is met when each has been deleted by the slice
  that made its assertion pass — not when it is relaxed, widened, or made non-strict.
- `FR-190`'s visual evidence is reproducible from the repository and deterministic across two runs,
  and no baseline, screenshot, or reference image is read as a source of a figure, route,
  capability, refusal reason, or governed word.
- No stylesheet rule this specification adds introduces a physical directional property.

The normal repository gates pass: `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and
