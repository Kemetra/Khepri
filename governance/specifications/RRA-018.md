# RRA-018: Reading the Governed Report in Place

**Authority:** this document governs from the commit in which the sole owner merges it to `main`
(`governance/CONSTITUTION.md` §II); before that merge it is a proposal. Its state is the
`governance/registry.yaml` row, not this line. It was drafted by automation, which "checks facts and
consistency; it does not approve changes or act as a separate authority" (§I).

Every code citation below was verified at `main` `7005be9`. Jira: `SCRUM-19`.

## Outcome

A reader who opens the governed web report, from the `/beta` report step or from Analysis detail in
`/app`, **reads it in the browser**. It is no longer saved as a file. At the top of the report they
see what it is, what it answered, and every individual result it was asked for and refused,
together with the reason. From any answered section they can step to that section's evidence and
back. The page runs no script, cannot be framed, and loads nothing from outside itself.

Nothing in this outcome is computed. Every figure, reason and name it presents is already on the
`ReportBundle` or in `RRA-009`'s vocabulary. What this specification changes is how the stored
report is **delivered** and how it is **composed**.

## Why this artifact exists

`SCRUM-19` asks for the minimum contract that lets an integrated report experience be built without
reopening the UI architecture. Checked against the tree, three of its six questions are real gaps
and three are already answered.

**Gap 1: every surface is served as a download, including the two the product says to open.**
`_artifact_response` (`src/khepri/rra/report_api.py:572-582`) sends
`Content-Disposition: attachment` for every artifact kind, and
`tests/test_rra006_report_artifact_api.py:99` pins it. Yet the shell labels those destinations
"Open report" and "Open evidence" (`src/khepri/runtime/shell_copy.py:164-165`), and the journey's
report step groups them as "the report and its evidence", apart from "other formats"
(`R8-10`, `tests/test_rra_journey_pages.py:186-203`). Clicking "Open report" saves a file. **No
active clause requires a download**: `governance/` names no `Content-Disposition` and no
attachment disposition for a report surface.

**Gap 2: a result refused on its own reaches no report surface.** `#531`'s owner decision
(2026-09-24) put the package's per-result refusals on the bundle as `ReportBundle.refusals`
(`src/khepri/rra/bundle.py:947-954`, `RRA-006`). Only the semantic-view projection reads them
(`src/khepri/rra/semantic_views/projection.py:612`); no renderer does. Rendering the golden package
(`tests/test_rra006_bundle.package()`) proves this. It refuses nine headline results, among them
gross margin, gross profit, cost and discount. Neither the English nor the Arabic business HTML, nor
either evidence surface, names any of the four. Four of the nine (`revenue_by_channel`,
`revenue_by_product`, `units_by_channel`, `units_by_product`) have no business name at all:
`wording.metric_business_name` raises `KeyError` for them (`src/khepri/rra/rendering/wording.py:238-240`).
That is a shortfall against `RRA-009`, which requires "a business metric name for every governed
metric code". The result-context refusal prose `RRA-009` requires exists
(`wording.refusal_message(..., context="result")`, `wording.py:888`), and no report surface calls it.

**Gap 3: the business report cannot reach its evidence.** The evidence surface is a separate
document by `RRA-009`'s design, and the business report does not link to it
(`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-03-r8-10-report-step-reading-vs-downloads-plan.md` §1). That plan
put the objection on citation identifiers, which are Audit-tier. A **section-level** link carries no
citation identifier and so does not meet that objection.

**Already answered, and not reopened here:**

- *Analytical order.* `ORDERED_SECTIONS` (`bundle.py:170-176`) fixes Overview → Comparison →
  Concentration → Growth → Basket, and the bundle refuses a language pair whose order differs.
- *Refused sections.* A refused section keeps its heading and states its reason
  (`report.html.j2:88-89`, `RRA-012`).
- *Shared Web/PDF template.* `report.pdf.html.j2` extends `report.html.j2` (`RRA-009`: "Render
  both regions through the existing shared template rather than forking the PDF surface").

**The family is `RRA`.** Every path this specification changes is under `src/khepri/rra/`. The
shell's labels already promise what Gap 1's fix delivers, so no `RCA` surface changes, and one
family governs the whole slice.

## Scope

- `src/khepri/rra/report_api.py`: `_artifact_response`, meaning the disposition and response headers
  per artifact kind. No route is added, renamed or removed.
- `src/khepri/rra/rendering/templates/report.html.j2`, `_components.html.j2`, `_evidence.html.j2`,
  `report.evidence.html.j2`, `report.pdf.html.j2`, `report.css` and `report.print.css`: the
  opening, the refused-results region, the section-to-evidence links and the return link.
- `src/khepri/rra/rendering/html.py`: context binding only. It passes `bundle.refusals` through
  existing wording, and adds the `FR-095a` chrome labels this specification names.
- `src/khepri/rra/rendering/wording.py`: the four missing business names (`FR-246`) and the
  chrome labels, in both languages. No description, synonym or meaning is authored.
- `tests/`: the tests §Verification names.

**Not in scope:** `src/khepri/rra/journey/` (`RRA-010`, `RRA-016`), `src/khepri/runtime/` and every
shell template (`RCA-002`, `RCA-005`, `RCA-010`), `rendering/excel.py`, `rendering/charts.py` and
`_chart.svg.j2` (`RRA-015`), `bundle.py` and `renderable.py` (`RRA-006`, `RRA-014`), the catalog
routes (`RRA-011`), and `migrations/`.

## Requirements

### Delivery

- **FR-240: Read in place.** `web_business_{language}` and `web_evidence_{language}` are served
  with `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="<name>"`, where `<name>` is the per-language name
  `_download_name` already produces (`#590`), so a reader who saves the page still gets the name
  that says its language. `pdf_{language}` and `excel` keep `attachment`. The routes, the session
  requirement, the refusal table and `Cache-Control: private, no-store` are unchanged.
- **FR-241: The page is walled.** Every inline response carries, in addition to the existing
  `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`:
  - `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; base-uri 'none';
    form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'; sandbox allow-same-origin`
  - `X-Frame-Options: DENY`
  - `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`

  `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` admits only the template's own `<style>` block. The stored page has no
  other stylesheet, and `default-src 'none'` refuses every fetch. No script can run, for two
  independent reasons: the policy has no `script-src`, and `sandbox` omits `allow-scripts`. The
  surface already bundles no JavaScript (`_components.html.j2:180`). `sandbox` keeps
  `allow-same-origin` because both session cookies are `SameSite=Strict`
  (`src/khepri/rra/api.py:176`, `src/khepri/rca/session_cookie.py:69`). A sandboxed page with an
  opaque origin would start same-site navigations that a browser may treat as cross-site, and the
  evidence link (`FR-250`) would lose the session. Without `allow-scripts`, `allow-same-origin`
  gives the page no capability it could use. The policy is exact: a slice that widens it (a
  `script-src`, an `img-src`, `allow-scripts`, `allow-popups`, any `frame-ancestors` source) is
  outside this specification.
- **FR-242: One stored document.** A reader who opens the report and a reader who saves it receive
  the same bytes. Delivery does not re-render, filter or rewrite the stored artifact (`RRA-009`: a
  presentation variant "is a render variant of each surface, and never a filter applied when a
  report is delivered").
- **FR-243: Stored reports keep their form.** An artifact materialized before the implementing
  slice merges is served inline under `FR-240`/`FR-241` exactly as it was stored, without the
  regions below. It is not re-rendered, backfilled or refused. The new composition reaches reports
  rendered after the merge.

### The opening

- **FR-244: Header metadata is a closed set.** The report header carries only the title, the
  governed automatic-generation disclosure, the report reference, and the `RRA-012` `FR-092`
  quality summary. The report reference is the one business-region identifier `RRA-009` permits,
  so rendering it in the header as well as the colophon adds no identifier. The header carries no
  file name, no uploaded content, no timestamp, no period, no session or job identifier, and no
  value the bundle does not already render elsewhere on the page.
- **FR-245: The index states each section's state.** The existing section index
  (`report.html.j2` `<nav>`) lists the sections in `ORDERED_SECTIONS` order, each with the
  `status_badge` its heading carries. It shows no figure. It does not reorder, select, promote or
  rank anything, and it carries no "headline figures" or "key results" region. The opening is
  composed of `FR-244`, this index and `FR-246`'s region, and of nothing else. No summary prose is
  authored. Narrative commentary, where a bundle carries it, stays where it renders today.

### Refused results

- **FR-246: Every refused result is named, with its reason.** When `bundle.refusals` is non-empty,
  the business region carries one region, headed by an `FR-095a` label, immediately after the
  header and before the index. It lists every entry in package order, each as the result's business
  name (`wording.metric_business_name`) followed by `RRA-009`'s result-context refusal prose for its
  reason (`wording.refusal_message(reason, context="result", language=...)`). Every name comes from
  the wording table, so the implementing slice adds business names for `revenue_by_channel`,
  `revenue_by_product`, `units_by_channel` and `units_by_product` in both languages. These are names
  only: no description, synonym or unsupported-interpretation text. A refused result with no
  business name refuses the render. It never renders its code. When `bundle.refusals` is empty,
  the region is absent.
- **FR-247: The region reads through the bundle only.** The region reads `bundle.refusals` through
  `RenderableBundle` and nothing else. It reads nothing from the package, per the #531 decision.
  It shows no count and no ordering other than package order, and it does not change the quality
  summary, whose counts remain counts of sections (`RRA-012` `FR-092`).
- **FR-248: The audit region states the codes.** The evidence surface lists the same refusals in
  the same order with the raw result code and the raw reason code. Raw codes appear only there
  (`RRA-009` §The business report).
- **FR-249: Family-scoped refusals are unchanged.** A result a family refuses inside its own
  present section still travels as that section's `<result>:<reason>` caveat (`bundle._scoped`)
  and renders where it renders today. The region is for the bundle's per-result refusals and does
  not duplicate those caveats.

### Summary, detail, evidence

- **FR-250: A section reaches its evidence.** Every present section of the web business surface
  carries one link to the evidence surface in the same language. The link targets the row for that
  section in the evidence surface's section-states table, which gains an anchor
  `id="evidence-section-{section_id}"`. The link is **relative** (`../evidence/{language}#evidence-section-{section_id}`),
  so the stored artifact embeds no job identifier, host or absolute route, and it carries no
  citation identifier. A refused section carries no such link. Its reason is already on the page.
- **FR-251: The evidence surface leads back.** The evidence surface carries one relative link to
  the business surface in the same language (`../web/{language}`), placed at its top.
- **FR-252: The relative links resolve only on their own routes.** `FR-250` and `FR-251` resolve
  against the report API's surface routes, which both the journey's links and the shell's handoff
  redirect reach. A saved copy of the page keeps its in-page anchors, and its cross-surface links
  do not resolve. That is accepted: the saved file is a record, not a session.

### Web and PDF

- **FR-253: One template, two outputs.** The header, index and refused-results region render in
  both the web and PDF outputs from `report.html.j2`, in both languages. The cross-surface links of
  `FR-250`/`FR-251` render on the web only: they sit in a block `report.pdf.html.j2` overrides
  empty, because a PDF cannot carry a session. In-page anchors remain in both.
- **FR-254: Parity and access.** Every label this specification introduces is an `FR-095a` chrome
  label in English and Arabic, and there is no language-specific branch. The refused-results
  region is a landmark named by its own heading, and uses logical properties only. Evidence links
  carry an accessible name that says which section's evidence they open.

## Exclusions

This specification does not authorize:

- any new route, redirect, query parameter, cookie, header other than `FR-241`'s, or change to the
  session or refusal model of the report API;
- any rendering of the report other than the stored artifact, or any composition of report content
  inside a shell or journey template;
- framing of any report surface, in any origin;
- any figure, total, count, ordering, selection, ranking, comparison, confidence, causal statement
  or recommendation beyond what the bundle already carries;
- any metric description, synonym, or explanation. `FR-246` adds names only;
- any change to `bundle.py`, `renderable.py`, `BUNDLE_VERSION`, the bundle identity, or what the
  package derives;
- the Excel surface. Its per-result refusals need their own slice, and until then the Excel surface
  is unchanged;
- any change to the journey's or the shell's copy, links or templates;
- a disposition change for `pdf_{language}` or `excel`.
