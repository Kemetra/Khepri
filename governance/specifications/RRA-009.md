# RRA-009: Business-First Report Presentation with Separated Audit Evidence

## Outcome

One report bundle presents the same facts through two regions on every surface: a business report a
retail owner can act on without knowing Khepri exists, and a governed audit-evidence layer carrying
every identifier. Both are generated for every report, in Arabic and English, across web, PDF, and
Excel. Neither is produced by filtering the other at delivery.

## Requirements

### Tiered classification

- Classify every field a surface renders as Business, Audit, or Internal.
- Treat a field as Business only if a retail owner could act on it without knowing Khepri exists;
  as Audit if it means something only to someone auditing Khepri; as Internal if it means something
  only to someone operating Khepri.
- Render Business fields in the primary report body on every report.
- Render Audit fields only in the separated audit region.
- Render Internal fields on no customer surface, including the audit region.
- Treat the twenty bundle-integrity reasons as Internal. When one fires no report is published, so
  a customer cannot encounter one in a delivered report and no customer-facing catalogue lists them.

### Separation across the three surfaces

- Carry the audit region as a distinct web page, as a PDF appendix following a page break, and as
  the final Excel worksheets ordered after every business worksheet.
- Generate the audit region with every report. Whether a customer's copy presents it is a render
  variant of each surface, and never a filter applied when a report is delivered.
- Render both regions through the existing shared template rather than forking the PDF surface.
- Name business worksheets by business meaning rather than from a section identifier.

### The business report

- Present a figure as a business statement of name and value, or as a chart with direct labels and
  no table where the row count allows, rather than as an identifier table.
- Provide a business metric name for every governed metric code. No translation path for a metric
  code exists today, so this vocabulary is new work rather than an extension of an existing one.
- Order the business report by decision relevance rather than by the mechanism that computed it.
- Carry exactly one identifier in the business region: a short human-readable report reference
  derived from the bundle identity.
- Carry the governed automatic-generation disclosure verbatim, and re-translate or shorten it
  nowhere.

### Refusals

- State a customer-facing refusal in five parts, in this order: which business analysis was
  unavailable, named as a capability rather than a section identifier; why the supplied data could
  not support it; whether the rest of the report remains valid; which missing field or evidence
  caused it, named as a column the customer would recognise in their own export; and how to make it
  available.
- State the third part explicitly on every refusal.
- Cover the whole customer-facing catalogue in both languages, in every context a code may refuse
  in. The requirement is total coverage of the catalogue as implemented, not a fixed count: a slice
  that adds a governed reason code adds its prose in both languages in the same slice, and the
  counts below move with it.
- As of this renewal the catalogue is eight section reasons and seven result reasons, being fifteen
  messages over thirteen distinct codes, with two codes appearing in both contexts. The two shared
  codes are `required_input_unavailable` and `incomplete_transaction_identifiers`.
- Carry no count in this specification that a reader cannot recompute from the source of truth.
  Section reasons are the `SECTION_REASON_*` constants and result reasons are `_RESULT_REASON_CODES`;
  a divergence between this paragraph and those sets is a defect in this paragraph.
- Carry the raw governed reason code only in the audit region.

### Caveats

- Provide customer prose for every caveat code. Language reconciliation compares caveat sets for
  equality rather than containment, so a business region that presents a friendly subset fails to
  reconcile.

### Citations

- Generate the figure-to-fact and passage-to-fact citation tables for every report, in the audit
  region.
- Present no claim in the business region that the audit region does not evidence. A report whose
  audit region is withheld from a customer's copy remains a cited export, because the citations are
  generated and reconciled rather than omitted.

### Identifiers in markup

- Retain an identifier in a markup attribute only where the reader uses it. A navigation anchor
  qualifies; a hook that serves tooling rather than a reader does not.
- Detect identifier leakage against rendered visible text rather than raw markup, so that a
  legitimate anchor is not reported as a leak.

### Preservation

- Relocate a field; never recompute one and never drop one.
- Recompute no figure in a renderer, and hold no decimal value there.
- Remove no figure, caveat, section, or disclosure from the bundle. Reconciliation compares the
  surface claim rather than the document, so relocation is claim-neutral and deletion is not.
- Add no chart kind.

## Exclusions

- White labeling, customer-authored templates, and customer-defined wording.
- Any new figure, aggregate, analysis family, metric, or chart kind.
- Any recomputation of a value inside a renderer.
- Delivery-time filtering of a required surface, and any delivery that does not name every required
  surface.
- Long-lived report history, persistent customer workspaces, and accumulation across sessions.
- Any change to the bundle binding, cross-surface reconciliation, language parity, or session
  expiry and deletion boundary that `RRA-006` fixes. This specification depends on `RRA-006` and
  amends none of it.

## Verification

Tests cover: every rendered field resolving to exactly one tier; the business and audit regions
together covering every figure, caveat, and section in the bundle, so that a relocation cannot
silently lose content; no Audit or Internal field appearing in visible business text on any surface
in either language; the audit region being generated even when a customer's copy omits it; a
delivery missing a required surface being refused; all thirteen refusal messages present in both
languages, each carrying five parts; every caveat code carrying prose in both languages; identifier
leakage measured against visible text while navigation anchors survive; business-region figures
being byte-identical to the strings the bundle produced; and deterministic regeneration.

Identity, lifecycle state, ownership, and approval evidence are authoritative in
`governance/registries/specifications.yaml`.
