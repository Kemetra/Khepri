# RCA-009: Period Comparison Runtime Composition

## Outcome

An authorized organization member can reach `PeriodComparisonView` through the existing semantic-
query composition root: the ordered subject and baseline sources are resolved inside one opaque
organization scope, assembled by the governed two-population source, and passed to the exact view
version without calculation, persistence, or fallback in the composition layer.

This specification resolves the `RCA-007` / `RCA-008` authority collision for that binding only.
It makes Period Comparison implementation actionable; it does not implement the binding or mark
Compare complete.

## Why this artifact exists

`RRA-014` publishes `PeriodComparisonView` with the exact
`governed two-population bundle` source shape. `RCA-008` publishes the Period Comparison surface,
but its open question, Exclusions, `FR-170`, and implementation precondition 2 deliberately refuse
to bind that source. `RCA-007` owns `src/khepri/runtime/semantic_view_adapter.py`, but its composition
flow rebuilds one `ReportBundle` for one run. The governed two-population facts and bundle already
exist under `RRA-008` and `RRA-006`, and `RCA-005` already governs the organization-scoped ordered
pair; the composition-root join from those authorities to the published view is the missing
authority.

The owner has ruled that M4 includes Compare. The alternative recorded in `RCA-008` -- accepting a
D1 milestone without Compare -- is not preserved as an available branch. M4 remains unmet until a
later implementation slice makes the source reachable and removes `FR-170`'s asserted absence.

## Successor reading

The earlier refusal is preserved as history and succeeded explicitly, not deleted or silently
weakened:

1. **`RCA-008` §The open question, paragraphs 2–3.** The statements that `RCA-008` does not claim
   the binding and that a successor composition artifact is a precondition remain the accurate
   reading of `RCA-008` alone. This specification is that successor and claims the binding described
   in §Scope below.
2. **`RCA-008` `FR-170`.** Its visible, asserted absence remains required until the source is
   reachable. It is re-read with this specification's `FR-179`: the implementation slice that
   makes the source reachable removes the assertion in the same slice. Merging this authority alone
   does not remove it.
3. **`RCA-008` §Exclusions.** “No cross-version or two-population binding” now means no such binding
   is authorized *under `RCA-008`*. It does not prohibit the one Period Comparison binding governed
   by this specification. Every other exclusion remains unchanged.
4. **`RCA-008` §Implementation preconditions, item 2.** “None may bind the two-population source
   under this document” remains true. The future reachability slice binds it under `RCA-009` and
   removes the `FR-170` assertion under the joint successor reading above.
5. **`RCA-007` §Scope and §Composition flow.** Its ownership of
   `semantic_view_adapter.py` and its single-population `ReportBundle.of(package)` flow remain the
   default for every view that admits that shape. For `PeriodComparisonView` only, this
   specification succeeds that flow at the bundle-selection step: the adapter may assemble the
   already-governed ordered pair into the existing two-population bundle and supply that bundle to
   projection. No other `RCA-007` flow, requirement, exclusion, or invariant is changed.

`RCA-007` and `RCA-008` therefore remain active. This is clause-level successor authority, not
whole-artifact retirement; neither may name `superseded_by` in the registry while active.

## Scope

- `src/khepri/runtime/semantic_view_adapter.py` -- one future composition-root branch that selects
  the governed two-population assembly only for `PeriodComparisonView` and supplies its result to
  the existing `RRA-014` projection protocol.
- The existing `FR-170` absence assertions and their explanatory comments in
  `src/khepri/rca/workspace/decision/`, `src/khepri/runtime/shell_controls.py`,
  `src/khepri/runtime/shell_decisions.py`, and the tests that assert the absence -- removal only, in
  the same future slice that makes the source reachable. No surface redesign is authorized.
- `tests/` -- focused reachability, source-shape, isolation, provenance, parity, no-write, and
  no-event evidence for that future binding.

The slice consumes the existing organization-scoped reads, ordered-pair orchestration,
two-population assembly, semantic-view definition, and Period Comparison surface. It does not
change `comparison_assembly.py`, a semantic-view definition, a calculation family, a bundle
contract, a route, or a surface read model beyond removing the asserted absence.

## Composition flow

```text
Request: exact PeriodComparisonView version + ordered subject/baseline source identifiers
RCA:     canonical authorization + two reads through one opaque organization scope
         -> existing governed two-population assembly over the ordered pair
         -> exact source-shape compatibility check, failing closed
RRA:     existing definition lookup + validation + no-calculation projection
Result:  existing refusal, empty outcome, or PeriodComparisonView projection, unchanged
```

An unavailable, deleted, corrupt, cross-scope, unordered, or incompatible source never falls back
to one population, a nearby pair, a different version, partial projection, or an empty result. The
governing authority for each outcome remains the authority that already defines it.

## Requirements

- **FR-172**: The new composition branch is selected only for an exact published version of
  `PeriodComparisonView` whose definition admits the governed two-population source shape. Every
  other view continues through `RCA-007` unchanged.
- **FR-173**: Canonical `RCA-001` authorization precedes both source reads. Subject and baseline are
  each loaded through the same requesting organization's opaque scope; absent, deleted, corrupt,
  or cross-scope inputs remain indistinguishable and yield `RCA-006` `FR-146`'s uniform,
  content-free unavailable outcome without identifying which source failed.
- **FR-174**: The adapter preserves the caller-supplied subject/baseline order and delegates
  assembly to the existing governed two-population path. It performs no arithmetic, aggregation,
  ordering inference, compatibility reconciliation, raw-row access, or new refusal derivation.
  Calculation and admission semantics remain exclusively `RRA-008`'s, and bundle semantics remain
  `RRA-006`'s.
- **FR-175**: Source-shape and pair compatibility fail closed before projection. A mismatch returns
  the existing governed refusal or unavailable outcome with no fallback, partial projection,
  single-population substitution, nearby-version substitution, or widening to unfiltered data.
- **FR-176**: Both source identities, operand order, retained bases, coverage identities, mapping,
  package, formula, family, bundle, and view versions, population qualifiers, evidence, caveats,
  and refusals survive composition and projection unchanged. The adapter adds, suppresses, or
  relabels none of them.
- **FR-177**: The reachable Period Comparison result preserves `RCA-008` `FR-164` and `FR-171`:
  governed refusal wording and visible content remain equivalent in Arabic and English, and neither
  language drops a figure, source identity, caveat, refusal, availability state, or evidence path.
- **FR-178**: The composed request remains derived at read time and non-materializing. It adds no
  database row, object, artifact, schema, migration, persisted projection, materialized view,
  pre-aggregation, sample, cache, preference, history entry, tombstone, retention change, audit
  event, product-telemetry event, counter, access record, or content-bearing log.
- **FR-179**: The implementation slice that first makes the governed two-population source
  reachable by `PeriodComparisonView` also removes every `FR-170` assertion and explanatory claim
  that Period Comparison is unreachable. The reachability proof distinguishes an admitted empty
  projection from `ViewRefusal('incompatible source shape')`; source reachability and absence
  removal may not ship separately.

## Exclusions

This specification grants no authority for a new route, public API, view, view version, surface,
read model, calculation, formula, metric, dimension, filter, refusal cause, bundle type, package
shape, persistence, schema, migration, cache, pre-aggregation, materialized view, sampling,
telemetry, audit record, retention rule, background job, export, sharing path, unrelated runtime
wiring, or broader composition redesign. It authorizes no three-population or arbitrary
cross-version composition and no change to unrelated routes or views.

## Retention, privacy, and telemetry

Unchanged from `RCA-006`, `RCA-007`, `RCA-008`, and `RRA-006`. Both sources remain inside one
authorized opaque organization scope. The bundle exists only for the request, is retained nowhere,
and creates no new record or event. Request parameters and result content enter no existing log.

`PeriodComparisonView` through the shipped composition root and no longer returns an incompatible-

refusals, availability, and evidence; every injected store records zero writes; no audit,
telemetry, counter, access record, or content-bearing log is emitted; and static scope evidence
shows that no unrelated view, route, or runtime binding changed.

The same slice removes the `FR-170` asserted-absence tests and comments named in §Scope and replaces
them with exercised reachability evidence. Every new file scores 10.00 in the required server-side
