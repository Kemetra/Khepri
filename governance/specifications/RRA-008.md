# RRA-008: Comparative and Concentration Retail Analysis

## Outcome

The fact package carries four families of deterministic derived facts — period comparison,
concentration, growth decomposition, and basket structure — each calculated from one compatible
`RRA-004` population and retained basis, reconciled to that basis, and refused rather than made
plausible when its preconditions fail.

## Period comparison

The governed comparison window is one period at the package's own day or month granularity.
Period-over-period uses the immediately preceding calendar period. Year-over-year uses the exact
same calendar period one year earlier. Nearest observed buckets never substitute for missing exact
counterparts.

Completeness and alignment come only from the authoritative `RRA-003` coverage manifest and the
`RRA-004` structural coverage signatures and separately retained daily bases:

- Complete full calendar periods are structurally compatible when they have the same governed
  aggregate scope or complete admitted store set and the same event-kind and status filters.
  Natural calendar length differences, including 28-, 29-, 30-, and 31-day months, do not make
  otherwise complete full periods incompatible.
- An incomplete current month may compare the contiguous prefix from day 1 through its last proven
  complete day `k` with the prior period's deterministic day-`1..k` structural coverage projection.
  The prior manifest must prove that entire prefix complete for the identical governed aggregate
  scope or complete admitted store set and event-kind and status filters. The two original monthly
  structural signatures need not carry identical ordinal sets.
- If there is no mapped store dimension, both manifests must name the same one store or the same
  already-governed aggregate scope.
- Attested store closure is complete zero activity. An extraction gap is not coverage.
- Sparse, non-contiguous, count-equal, gap-containing, scope-mismatched, store-mismatched, or
  filter-mismatched structures refuse. Equal counts, observed rows, date bounds, and generated
  date spines never prove alignment.
- Day-granularity comparison requires an exact date counterpart and complete admitted-store
  coverage on both days.
- Leap-day YoY refuses when the prior calendar has no exact counterpart.

Facts disclose both aligned start and end dates, the compatible structural coverage fields and
signatures, population, and separately retained daily measure bases. Partial-prefix comparison
evidence records the accepted current prefix signature and basis, the prior projected signature
and basis, and every preserved parent signature and basis identity. It carries the bilingual
partial-window caveat required by `RRA-009`. Without an authoritative valid manifest, observed
trends may survive but completeness-dependent comparison and growth refuse.

For current value `current` and exact compatible prior value `prior`:

- absolute delta is `current - prior` and survives a zero or negative prior base;
- percentage delta is `(current - prior) / prior` and refuses unless `prior > 0`;
- absence of the exact governed counterpart or a compatible population refuses the absolute delta
  and therefore the percentage delta.

## Growth decomposition

Growth consumes the exact PoP window selected by period comparison and may not select another.
Both aligned windows must be return-free posted-sale populations over
`sales_complete_revenue_units`, with complete revenue, strictly positive units, the structural
coverage compatibility already accepted by comparison, proven currency, and no unmatched eligible
row. A return, missing row, non-positive unit denominator, unproven currency, or rejected structural
compatibility refuses growth. Growth uses comparison's accepted aligned daily measure bases and
does not compare measure-bearing signatures or require daily revenue or unit values to be equal.

For current sale revenue `R_c`, prior sale revenue `R_p`, current sale units `U_c`, prior sale
units `U_p`, and `ASP = R / U`, with no intermediate rounding:

```
volume effect = ASP_p * (U_c - U_p)
price effect  = U_c * (ASP_c - ASP_p)
revenue delta = R_c - R_p = volume effect + price effect
```

The entire interaction term is assigned to price. The machine metric remains `price_effect`, but
its business label is **realized price/mix effect** because aggregate ASP also changes with product
mix. Every surface carries equal Arabic and English caveats that this is not a pure same-SKU price
change. The allocation is a governed commercial convention; exact unrounded additivity is an
algebraic invariant.

Published round-half-even values preserve displayed reconciliation exactly:

```
published revenue delta = round(R_c - R_p)
published volume effect = round(unrounded volume effect)
published price effect  = published revenue delta - published volume effect
rounding residual       = published price effect - round(unrounded price effect)
```

Audit evidence records the rounding residual. It is assigned to price and must be no larger than
one unit of the published last place; a larger residual is a reconciliation failure.

## Basket structure

Items per transaction and attach rate use complete posted-sale populations only. Returns, voids,
and cancelled events enter neither numerator nor denominator.

Items per transaction is:

```
sum(positive posted-sale units)
------------------------------------------
count_distinct(canonical sale transaction key)
```

It uses `sales_complete_units_transactions`, includes free or bonus items, and refuses missing or
non-integral units, incomplete canonical transaction keys, or a denominator `<= 0`.

For each governed product or category value, attach rate is:

```
distinct posted sale transactions containing value
--------------------------------------------------
all distinct posted sale transactions in the same dimension-complete population
```

The denominator is the exact distinct canonical transaction set in
`dimension_complete_sales:<product|category>`, never rows, units, revenue, displayed buckets, or a
different transaction population. Multiple lines of one value in a transaction count once. Every
eligible sale row must carry the dimension value; one missing value refuses that dimension's
entire attach family rather than silently entering only the denominator. Missing or colliding
canonical keys or a denominator `<= 0` also refuse.

`other`, `unlabelled`, and `redacted` synthetic display buckets never receive attach rate. Display
truncation changes no numerator or denominator. Product and category attach families are admitted
independently and neither suppresses the other.

## Sales revenue concentration

Concentration ranks posted-sale revenue over the full, non-null, admissible product or category
set with complete sale revenue, before display truncation. Its business name is **sales revenue
concentration**. It reconciles to `dimension_sales_revenue_basis:<dimension>`. When returns exist,
every surface states that this retained sale-revenue basis differs from return-inclusive headline
revenue. Concentration refuses when the full distinct set cannot be computed within admissibility
limits.

A missing dimension on any eligible posted sale, including a zero-revenue row, refuses that
dimension. `None` and synthetic `unlabelled` are never ranked. Any negative ranked value or a
non-positive total ranked revenue refuses the curve. Zero-revenue values remain distinct, rank
last, and form a flat tail. They remain in distinct count `n`, affect the discrete cutoff, and have
their count disclosed in audit evidence and the business caveat.

The authoritative full curve contains cumulative revenue divided by total ranked revenue. Top
decile share is the curve share after `ceil(n / 10)` ranked values, and top quartile share is the
curve share after `ceil(n / 4)` ranked values, with at least one value in either cutoff. Shares use
four decimal places. The ceiling and zero-value conventions are part of the concentration formula
version. The fact package records the full-set distinct-value count and the count actually ranked,
and assigns no fixed classification bands to either measured share.

The full curve remains authoritative. Presentation-only sampling keeps no more than 100 points,
including the final 100% point, and carries a bilingual sampling caveat. Sampling never changes a
calculation, numerator, denominator, rank, or cutoff.

## Cross-cutting provenance and survival

Each derived fact and series discloses readable formula and mapping versions, population code,
event-kind and status filters, currency for monetary facts, aligned window, compatible retained
bases, precision, decimal round-half-even mode, caveats, fact identity, and citation identity.
Reruns with identical input and governed versions are byte-equivalent.

Arabic and English surfaces carry equal facts, caveats, refusals, and citations. Every later code
slice that adds a refusal or caveat must add its complete customer wording in both languages in
the same slice under `RRA-009`.

## Exclusions

- Forecasting, trend extrapolation, and projected values.
- Customer-defined metrics and customer-authored formulas.
- Generic non-retail analysis and metrics outside the governed retail vocabulary.
- Cohort, retention, and repeat-purchase analysis. No governed customer identifier is admitted;
  this is a permanent boundary, not deferred work.
- Breakdowns crossing two dimensions simultaneously.
- New mapping, admissibility, package-shape, or formula semantics other than the exact
  `rra003.mapping.v3`, `rra004.package.v3`, and `rra004.formula.v2` changes governed by `RRA-003`
  and `RRA-004` and consumed here.
