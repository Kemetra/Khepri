# RRA-008: Comparative and Concentration Retail Analysis

## Outcome

The fact package carries four families of deterministic derived facts — period comparison,
concentration, growth decomposition, and basket structure — each calculated from one compatible
`RRA-004` population and retained basis, reconciled to that basis, and refused rather than made
plausible when its preconditions fail.

A fifth family, **two-population comparison**, is calculated from exactly two compatible
`RRA-004` populations under §Two-population comparison. It is the only family that reads more than
one population, and it derives nothing until every admission predicate there holds. The four
single-population families are unchanged by its addition.

## Period comparison

The governed comparison window is one period at the package's own day or month granularity.
Period-over-period uses the immediately preceding calendar period. Year-over-year uses the exact
same calendar period one year earlier. Nearest observed buckets never substitute for missing exact
counterparts.

Completeness and alignment come only from the authoritative `RRA-003` coverage manifest and the
`RRA-004` structural coverage signatures and separately retained daily bases:

- Complete full calendar periods are structurally compatible when they have the same governed
  aggregate scope or complete admitted store set and the same event-kind and status filters.
  Natural calendar length differences, including 28-, 29-, 30-, and 31-day months, do not make
  otherwise complete full periods incompatible.
- An incomplete current month may compare the contiguous prefix from day 1 through its last proven
  complete day `k` with the prior period's deterministic day-`1..k` structural coverage projection.
  The prior manifest must prove that entire prefix complete for the identical governed aggregate
  scope or complete admitted store set and event-kind and status filters. The two original monthly
  structural signatures need not carry identical ordinal sets.
- If there is no mapped store dimension, both manifests must name the same one store or the same
  already-governed aggregate scope.
- Attested store closure is complete zero activity. An extraction gap is not coverage.
- Sparse, non-contiguous, count-equal, gap-containing, scope-mismatched, store-mismatched, or
  filter-mismatched structures refuse. Equal counts, observed rows, date bounds, and generated
  date spines never prove alignment.
- Day-granularity comparison requires an exact date counterpart and complete admitted-store
  coverage on both days.
- Leap-day YoY refuses when the prior calendar has no exact counterpart.

Facts disclose both aligned start and end dates, the compatible structural coverage fields and
signatures, population, and separately retained daily measure bases. Partial-prefix comparison
evidence records the accepted current prefix signature and basis, the prior projected signature
and basis, and every preserved parent signature and basis identity. It carries the bilingual
partial-window caveat required by `RRA-009`. Without an authoritative valid manifest, observed
trends may survive but completeness-dependent comparison and growth refuse.

For current value `current` and exact compatible prior value `prior`:

- absolute delta is `current - prior` and survives a zero or negative prior base;
- percentage delta is `(current - prior) / prior` and refuses unless `prior > 0`;
- absence of the exact governed counterpart or a compatible population refuses the absolute delta
  and therefore the percentage delta.

## Growth decomposition

Growth consumes the exact PoP window selected by period comparison and may not select another.
Both aligned windows must be return-free posted-sale populations over
`sales_complete_revenue_units`, with complete revenue, strictly positive units, the structural
coverage compatibility already accepted by comparison, proven currency, and no unmatched eligible
row. A return, missing row, non-positive unit denominator, unproven currency, or rejected structural
compatibility refuses growth. Growth uses comparison's accepted aligned daily measure bases and
does not compare measure-bearing signatures or require daily revenue or unit values to be equal.

For current sale revenue `R_c`, prior sale revenue `R_p`, current sale units `U_c`, prior sale
units `U_p`, and `ASP = R / U`, with no intermediate rounding:

```
volume effect = ASP_p * (U_c - U_p)
price effect  = U_c * (ASP_c - ASP_p)
revenue delta = R_c - R_p = volume effect + price effect
```

The entire interaction term is assigned to price. The machine metric remains `price_effect`, but
its business label is **realized price/mix effect** because aggregate ASP also changes with product
mix. Every surface carries equal Arabic and English caveats that this is not a pure same-SKU price
change. The allocation is a governed commercial convention; exact unrounded additivity is an
algebraic invariant.

Published round-half-even values preserve displayed reconciliation exactly:

```
published revenue delta = round(R_c - R_p)
published volume effect = round(unrounded volume effect)
published price effect  = published revenue delta - published volume effect
rounding residual       = published price effect - round(unrounded price effect)
```

Audit evidence records the rounding residual. It is assigned to price and must be no larger than
one unit of the published last place; a larger residual is a reconciliation failure.

## Basket structure

Items per transaction and attach rate use complete posted-sale populations only. Returns, voids,
and cancelled events enter neither numerator nor denominator.

Items per transaction is:

```
sum(positive posted-sale units)
------------------------------------------
count_distinct(canonical sale transaction key)
```

It uses `sales_complete_units_transactions`, includes free or bonus items, and refuses missing or
non-integral units, incomplete canonical transaction keys, or a denominator `<= 0`.

For each governed product or category value, attach rate is:

```
distinct posted sale transactions containing value
--------------------------------------------------
all distinct posted sale transactions in the same dimension-complete population
```

The denominator is the exact distinct canonical transaction set in
`dimension_complete_sales:<product|category>`, never rows, units, revenue, displayed buckets, or a
different transaction population. Multiple lines of one value in a transaction count once. Every
eligible sale row must carry the dimension value; one missing value refuses that dimension's
entire attach family rather than silently entering only the denominator. Missing or colliding
canonical keys or a denominator `<= 0` also refuse.

`other`, `unlabelled`, and `redacted` synthetic display buckets never receive attach rate. Display
truncation changes no numerator or denominator. Product and category attach families are admitted
independently and neither suppresses the other.

## Sales revenue concentration

Concentration ranks posted-sale revenue over the full, non-null, admissible product or category
set with complete sale revenue, before display truncation. Its business name is **sales revenue
concentration**. It reconciles to `dimension_sales_revenue_basis:<dimension>`. When returns exist,
every surface states that this retained sale-revenue basis differs from return-inclusive headline
revenue. Concentration refuses when the full distinct set cannot be computed within admissibility
limits.

A missing dimension on any eligible posted sale, including a zero-revenue row, refuses that
dimension. `None` and synthetic `unlabelled` are never ranked. Any negative ranked value or a
non-positive total ranked revenue refuses the curve. Zero-revenue values remain distinct, rank
last, and form a flat tail. They remain in distinct count `n`, affect the discrete cutoff, and have
their count disclosed in audit evidence and the business caveat.

The authoritative full curve contains cumulative revenue divided by total ranked revenue. Top
decile share is the curve share after `ceil(n / 10)` ranked values, and top quartile share is the
curve share after `ceil(n / 4)` ranked values, with at least one value in either cutoff. Shares use
four decimal places. The ceiling and zero-value conventions are part of the concentration formula
version. The fact package records the full-set distinct-value count and the count actually ranked,
and assigns no fixed classification bands to either measured share.

The full curve remains authoritative. Presentation-only sampling keeps no more than 100 points,
including the final 100% point, and carries a bilingual sampling caveat. Sampling never changes a
calculation, numerator, denominator, rank, or cutoff.

## Two-population comparison

This family compares **two dataset versions**, where the four families above compare two periods
inside one. That is not a new calculation but a new admission: the only family whose input is more
than one `RRA-004` population. It authorizes the scope `G4-01` defines and nothing beyond it.

A two-population comparison fact carries the same `Fact` shape as every other governed number —
no new type, no new field. Its governed formula version is **`rra008.crossversion.v1`**, which is
a distinct derivation from `rra008.comparison.v2` and must never be recorded as, or reinterpreted
under, that version: a cross-version delta and a period-over-period delta answer different
questions, and a fact that cannot say which one produced it is not provenance.

### Admission

Six predicates. **All six must hold before any figure is derived.** Any failure refuses the pair
with the stated cause and derives nothing — no partial, indicative, or best-effort figure.

| # | Predicate | Refusal cause |
|---|---|---|
| **D-1** | Both versions belong to one organization scope | cross-organization |
| **D-2** | Identical `rra003.mapping` version | mapping drift |
| **D-3** | Identical `rra004.formula` version | formula drift |
| **D-4** | Identical `rra004.package` shape version | package drift |
| **D-5** | Same currency, and the same governed aggregate scope or the **identical** complete admitted store set for both versions, plus identical event-kind and status filters | incomparable basis, scope mismatch, or store mismatch |
| **D-6** | Both versions' coverage is complete full calendar periods for their stated period, per the authoritative `RRA-003` coverage manifest | incomplete coverage |

`D-2` through `D-4` refuse rather than reconcile. Two populations measured under different
mapping, formula, or package versions were measured differently, and a delta across that boundary
is a number with no interpretation — a stricter rule than this specification already applies to a
merely incomplete counterpart period, not a looser one.

`D-5` requires the store set be **identical**, not merely complete on both sides. Two sets that
are each complete and different from each other are a breakdown across one population's dimension
members, which is `RRA-004`'s, and this specification already refuses store-mismatched structures
in its single-population comparison.

### Operand order

The pair is **ordered, and the order is an input rather than a derivation.** One version is the
**subject** and the other the **baseline**; the requesting slice states which, and this
specification does not infer it from sealing time, identifier, or period.

For subject value `subject` and baseline value `baseline`, and consistent with §Period comparison's
existing definitions:

- absolute delta is `subject - baseline` and survives a zero or negative baseline;
- percentage delta is `(subject - baseline) / baseline` and refuses unless `baseline > 0`.

An unordered request is refused with cause **unordered pair**. This specification states no default
order, because choosing one would be an orchestration decision it does not own.

### Composite provenance

The four single-population families each read one population, so `Fact.inputs` — a list of governed
semantic measure names — together with the package's own population code identifies what a fact was
derived from. **That is not sufficient here**: `Fact.inputs` names measures, not dataset versions,
so it cannot say which two populations a two-population fact compared, nor in which order.

This family therefore discloses, in addition to everything §Cross-cutting provenance and survival
already requires of every fact:

- the **subject** dataset version's opaque identifier and its cited compatible retained basis;
- the **baseline** dataset version's opaque identifier and its cited compatible retained basis;
- the operand order, such that subject and baseline are distinguishable and not merely a set;
- both versions' coverage-manifest identities, since `D-6` is asserted against each separately.

`Fact.inputs` keeps its existing meaning and is not repurposed to carry identifiers, and the
`RRA-004` `Fact` type is not modified: this is a disclosure requirement on the family, and the slice
that first serializes these facts states how it is carried. **Operand order is part of fact
identity** — the same pair with the order reversed is a different fact with a different identity,
not the same fact negated — so a fact that cannot disclose its order does not state what it
measured, and reruns are byte-equivalent only for the same ordered pair.

### Period rule

The two versions' periods are **compared as stated, never aligned.** This family introduces no
truncation, no rescaling, and no per-day normalization. A pair is refused when the periods differ
in granularity or retail-day boundary, or when either period is incomplete.

A **natural calendar length difference is not a refusal.** Natural calendar length differences,
including 28-, 29-, 30-, and 31-day months, do not make otherwise complete full periods
incompatible, exactly as §Period comparison already provides; February against March is the
ordinary case of this family.

What is declined is this specification's **prefix concession**, not its calendar tolerance. An
incomplete current month may compare its contiguous day-`1..k` prefix against the prior period's
day-`1..k` projection within one population's retained daily bases. Across two dataset versions
that would require reconciling two coverage manifests, which no active artifact authorizes — so
`D-6` requires both periods complete and the prefix case is outside this family.

### Refusals and caveats

The causes named in §Admission and §Operand order — cross-organization, mapping drift, formula
drift, package drift, incomparable basis, scope mismatch, store mismatch, incomplete coverage,
unordered pair, and the granularity and retail-day-boundary refusals of §Period rule — are the
complete set this family introduces. **No later slice may add a refusal cause to this family
without amending this specification.**

Each carries its complete customer wording in **both Arabic and English in the same code slice
that raises it**, under `RRA-009` and §Cross-cutting provenance and survival, which already states
this for every family. Consistent with every specification in this repository, the customer strings
themselves are not written here: a specification states the parity obligation and the code slice
supplies the wording. A refusal shipped in one language only is incomplete, and §Verification's
bilingual parity clause covers this family as it covers the other four.

### Frozen contracts

The four contracts `C1` builds against are **frozen at this section's text and `RCA-005`
§Comparison orchestration's**, and are stated here by reference rather than restated, so that one
document cannot drift from the other. A `C1` slice may consume them; a `C1` slice may not widen
them, and one that needs a change needs an owner-merged amendment first.

**Input and Compatibility span both specifications, and an amendment to either half must carry the
other in the same owner-merged change.** The pair's shape, ordering and admission live here; who
may submit it lives in `RCA-005` `FR-130`/`FR-131`. Amending one alone would leave two frozen
contracts describing different things, which is the drift this freeze exists to prevent — so a
change to the ordered pair, to `D-1`'s scope rule, or to the refusal-without-figure rule is a
single amendment touching both documents, never two independent ones. Output and Evidence are
wholly this specification's, and retention and audit wholly `RCA-005`'s; those halves may be
amended alone.

| Contract | Frozen as | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Input** | An ordered pair — subject and baseline, supplied by the caller, never inferred — of two dataset versions in one organization scope | §Operand order, §Admission `D-1`, `RCA-005` `FR-130` |
| **Output** | A `Fact` of the existing `RRA-004` shape under `rra008.crossversion.v1`, with `subject - baseline` deltas and the percentage form refusing unless `baseline > 0` | §Operand order, §Stable contract and versions |
| **Compatibility** | The six predicates `D-1`–`D-6`, all of which must hold, plus §Period rule's granularity, retail-day-boundary and completeness conditions | §Admission, §Period rule |
| **Evidence** | Both versions' identifiers, both cited retained bases, both coverage-manifest identities, and the operand order — with order part of fact identity | §Composite provenance |

**The filter contract is frozen at identity, not at an enumeration.** `D-5` requires the two
versions carry **identical** event-kind and status filters, so this family never needs to know
which filters are supported: the admissible set is exactly whatever `RRA-003` and `RRA-004` admit
for a single population, and any difference between the pair refuses. This specification therefore
defines **no filter vocabulary of its own**, and a slice that introduces one is outside it.

**Neither filter case is a new refusal cause**, and the frozen set below stays complete. They are
two existing outcomes, stated here so the boundary is observable rather than inferred:

- A filter **`RRA-004` does not admit** fails that specification's admission *before* a pair is
  ever considered. No population is built, so no two-population comparison is requested and this
  family emits nothing — the refusal is `RRA-003`/`RRA-004`'s, under its own wording.
- Two filters **each admitted but differing between the versions** fail `D-5`, with its existing
  cause `incomparable basis` — the same cause a currency or scope difference raises, because it is
  the same defect: the two populations do not describe comparable things.

The refusal-cause set of §Refusals and caveats is frozen with them: complete as listed, and not
extendable by a later slice without amending this specification.

### Not authorized here

- **Three or more populations.** Pairwise only. N-way comparison is deferred and is not this
  family's.
- **Any change to `RRA-004`.** The population contract, the `Fact` type, and its provenance fields
  stand unchanged; this family consumes them.
- **Orchestration, persistence, or a customer surface.** Who may request a comparison, what is
  retained, and where it is shown are not this specification's. It states what a fact is and when
  it refuses.
- **Reinterpretation of historical facts.** Facts recorded under `rra008.comparison.v2` remain
  that family's and are not re-derived as cross-version facts.

## Cross-cutting provenance and survival

Each derived fact and series discloses readable formula and mapping versions, population code,
event-kind and status filters, currency for monetary facts, aligned window, compatible retained
bases, precision, decimal round-half-even mode, caveats, fact identity, and citation identity.
Reruns with identical input and governed versions are byte-equivalent.

Every family reconciles to its cited `RRA-004` basis. A failure or missing optional input refuses
only dependent results, leaving independently answerable facts and the rest of the report intact.
Arabic and English surfaces carry equal facts, caveats, refusals, and citations. Every later code
slice that adds a refusal or caveat must add its complete customer wording in both languages in
the same slice under `RRA-009`.

## Stable contract and versions

- `rra008.comparison.v2` succeeds `rra008.comparison.v1`.
- `rra008.growth.v2` succeeds `rra008.growth.v1`.
- `rra008.basket.v2` succeeds `rra008.basket.v1`.
- `rra008.concentration.v2` succeeds `rra008.concentration.v1`.
- `rra008.crossversion.v1` is new and succeeds nothing. It governs §Two-population comparison
  only, and is not a successor to `rra008.comparison.v2`, which continues to govern
  period comparison within one population.

Historical derived facts and packages remain immutable under their recorded versions and are not
reinterpreted.

## Exclusions

- Forecasting, trend extrapolation, and projected values.
- Customer-defined metrics and customer-authored formulas.
- Generic non-retail analysis and metrics outside the governed retail vocabulary.
- Cohort, retention, and repeat-purchase analysis. No governed customer identifier is admitted;
  this is a permanent boundary, not deferred work.
- Breakdowns crossing two dimensions simultaneously.
- New mapping, admissibility, package-shape, or formula semantics other than the exact
  `rra003.mapping.v3`, `rra004.package.v3`, and `rra004.formula.v2` changes governed by `RRA-003`
  and `RRA-004` and consumed here. `rra008.crossversion.v1` is this specification's own derivation
  version, declared in §Stable contract and versions as the other four `rra008.*` versions are,
  and it introduces no `RRA-003` or `RRA-004` semantics: §Two-population comparison `D-2`–`D-4`
  require those versions be **identical** across the pair.
- Comparison of three or more `RRA-004` populations. §Two-population comparison is pairwise, and
  N-way comparison is deferred rather than authorized here.
- Orchestration, persistence, retention, and customer surfaces for any family, including
  §Two-population comparison. This specification states what a fact is and when it refuses.

## Verification

Golden and adversarial tests cover exact PoP and YoY counterparts; complete, partial-prefix,
sparse, gap, closure, aggregate-scope, store-mismatch, and leap-day coverage; absolute and
percentage bases; return-free matched growth and its residual; sale-only complete basket
populations and canonical distinct denominators; full non-null concentration including zero-value
tails, negative and non-positive refusals, ceiling cutoffs, and sampling; independent result
survival; bilingual caveat and refusal parity; reconciliation; citation and readable provenance;
historical-version preservation; and deterministic reruns.

For §Two-population comparison, tests additionally cover each of `D-1` through `D-6` refusing with
its stated cause and deriving nothing; a natural calendar length difference between two complete
full periods being admitted rather than refused; granularity, retail-day-boundary, and incomplete
period pairs refused; a store set complete on both sides but not identical refused under `D-5`;
`rra008.crossversion.v1` recorded on every fact of this family and never confused with
`rra008.comparison.v2`; an unordered request refused; the same pair in reversed order yielding a
distinct fact identity rather than a negated value, with both orders' facts disclosing which
version was subject and which baseline alongside both retained bases and both coverage-manifest
identities; byte-equivalent reruns for the same ordered pair; and bilingual parity for every
refusal cause and caveat the family adds.
