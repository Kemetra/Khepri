# RRA-008: Comparative and Concentration Retail Analysis

## Outcome

The fact package carries four families of deterministic derived facts — period comparison,
concentration, growth decomposition, and basket structure — each reconciled to the `RRA-004`
aggregates it derives from, and each refusing rather than misleading when its preconditions fail.

## Requirements

### Period comparison

- Compare a current window to a prior window of equal length, for period-over-period and
  year-over-year.
- Truncate both windows to the same day count when the current window is incomplete, and emit a
  caveat naming the truncated window on every surface in both languages.
- Refuse the affected comparison, and not the report, when the prior window has no coverage.
- Emit absolute deltas always, and refuse the percentage delta when the base is zero or negative.
- Follow the governed time granularity already fixed for trends.

### Concentration

- Rank products or categories by revenue and emit the cumulative share curve.
- Compute concentration over the full admissible distinct-value set, never over the truncated
  display buckets, and record the distinct-value count and the count actually ranked.
- Refuse when the full distinct set cannot be computed within admissibility limits.
- Emit the measured revenue share held by the top decile and the top quartile of ranked values,
  and assign no fixed classification bands.

### Growth decomposition

- Split a revenue change between price and volume as
  `(average_selling_price_prior * units_change) + (units_current * average_selling_price_change)`.
- Require the two parts to sum exactly to the revenue change, and treat any inequality as a
  reconciliation failure.
- Record that the interaction term is assigned to price, and pin the formula version.
- Refuse when units are zero in either period.

### Basket structure

- Emit items per transaction, and attach rate as the share of transactions containing a given
  admissible dimension value.
- Require a mapped transaction identifier for both, refuse with a stated reason when it is absent,
  and never substitute row count for transaction count.
- Require an admissible product or category dimension for attach rate.

### Cross-cutting

- Reconcile every derived fact to its source aggregate, and emit a caveat or refuse the affected
  result when reconciliation fails.
- Assign stable fact and citation identifiers, and record input digest, mapping version, formula
  version, dimensions, filters, units, precision, and caveats.
- Make reruns with identical input and governed versions byte-equivalent.
- Carry equal facts, caveats, and citations in Arabic and English.

## Exclusions

- Forecasting, trend extrapolation, and any projected value.
- Customer-defined metrics and customer-authored formulas.
- Generic non-retail analysis and metrics outside the governed retail vocabulary.
- Cohort, retention, and repeat-purchase analysis. The governed mapping carries no customer
  identifier, deliberately, because likely personal-data columns are detected and excluded from
  narrative and reporting inputs. This is a permanent boundary, not a deferred feature.
- Breakdowns crossing two dimensions simultaneously.
- New mapping semantics, and any change to the profiling, admissibility, or fact-package
  specifications this one builds on.

## Verification

Golden-dataset tests covering: like-for-like truncation and its bilingual caveat; refusal on absent
prior-window coverage; zero and negative percentage bases; missing transaction identifier;
line-item-grain input not being mistaken for transaction grain; decomposition additivity asserted as
an exact equality rather than a tolerance; concentration computed over the full distinct set while
the display truncates; citation and provenance integrity; and deterministic rerun.

This specification is draft and does not authorize product implementation.
