# RCA-004: Public Product Landing Surface

## Outcome

Khepri may expose one bilingual public product landing surface that explains the governed retail
decision product, demonstrates its trust model with clearly labelled synthetic examples, and links
only to destinations already authorized by active governance.

The landing is presentation and pre-purchase communication only. It creates no authentication,
signup, billing, analytics, telemetry, retention, or new analytical behavior.

## Requirements

Numbering continues from `RCA-003`, which ends at `FR-080`.

### Surface and content boundary

- **FR-081**: Khepri MAY render exactly one public, non-authenticated product-marketing surface.
  It MUST explain only existing governed product behavior and MUST NOT disclose customer-specific
  state.
- **FR-082**: The landing MUST render English and Arabic as first-class equivalent surfaces, with
  correct `lang` and `dir` metadata and RTL for Arabic. Its styles MUST use logical rather than
  physical directional properties.
- **FR-083**: The landing MAY include its route, template, and bundled/local assets. It MAY use the
  approved Register Wall / Ancient-Egypt-inspired design direction recorded by PR #335. That design
  concept is design direction, not a second source of authority, and does not govern the private
  beta application.
- **FR-084**: Demonstration data MUST be synthetic and clearly labelled as synthetic. It MUST NOT
  be presented as customer evidence.
- **FR-085**: The landing MAY summarize governed trust concepts, including the design labels
  proven, caveated, or withheld, only where their customer-facing meaning remains consistent with
  `RRA-009` and `RRA-011`. Marketing terminology MUST yield to those RRA authorities on conflict.
  The landing MUST NOT introduce a duplicate manually maintained truth for metrics, reasons,
  caveats, or their meanings.
- **FR-086**: The landing MAY link to an implemented `RCA-003` legal/trust page. It MUST NOT add
  the landing to `RCA-003`'s closed legal/trust inventory or alter that inventory merely to reuse
  it.

### CTA boundary

- **FR-087**: The landing MAY render one CTA only when its destination already exists in the
  runtime and is authorized by active governance. An existing authorized request-access destination
  or a beta entry path that legitimately supports a public interaction are examples; this
  specification authorizes neither destination.
- **FR-088**: The CTA destination MUST be chosen from runtime truth, not marketing preference. No
  dead CTA, disabled "Coming Soon" CTA, invented route, future signup flow, or public signup may
  render.

### Presentation and security

- **FR-089**: The landing MUST support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, appropriate text and
  non-text contrast, and state communication that does not rely on color alone.
- **FR-090**: The landing MUST use bundled/local assets only. It MUST NOT introduce external fonts,
  CDN runtime assets, third-party scripts, trackers, analytics cookies, advertising, inline scripts,
  or inline styles where the existing public CSP or policy forbids them.
- **FR-091**: The landing MAY remain a visually distinct marketing world from the private-beta
  application. No private-beta journey route, template, asset, or visual language need change to
  match it.

## Exclusions

This specification does not authorize:

- public signup, account creation, authentication changes, or organization or membership behavior;
- billing, prices, subscriptions, payment providers, invoices, refunds, or credits;
- telemetry, trackers, analytics cookies, advertising, customer-data collection, retention, or
  persistence changes;
- new API capability, RRA calculation or analysis capability, report semantics, refusal or caveat
  meaning, confidence score, or quality score;
- fabricated customers, logos, testimonials, certifications, compliance claims, or ROI claims;
- external fonts, CDN runtime assets, or third-party scripts;
- changes to the private-beta journey merely to visually match the landing; or
- changes to `RCA-003`'s closed legal page inventory unless a real implementation dependency proves
  one necessary.

## RRA boundary

`RRA-009` remains the authority for governed refusal, caveat, and customer-facing wording
semantics. `RRA-011` remains the authority for metric, trust, and catalog vocabulary where it
applies. Marketing copy may summarize governed product behavior but does not redefine it. A
synthetic example is illustrative only and never actual customer evidence.

## Implementation slice

**LAND1-01 — Public product landing**

Purpose: implement the approved public landing concept under this specification after `RCA-004`
is active on `main`.

Expected scope: a public landing route; bilingual template and copy; landing-specific stylesheet and
local assets; wiring into existing public runtime; links to implemented legal/trust surfaces; and
tests for route, language parity, accessibility, and security boundaries.

This specification authorizes no implementation in this change.

## Verification

- Tests prove the landing is public, non-authenticated, bilingual, RTL-correct, keyboard usable,
  reduced-motion aware, and does not communicate meaning through color alone.
- Tests prove synthetic examples are labelled and no landing copy presents them as customer evidence.
- Structural tests prove no third-party runtime asset, tracker, analytics cookie, inline script, or
  prohibited inline style is introduced.
- CTA tests prove at most one CTA renders and every rendered destination exists and is authorized;
  no dead, disabled, or invented CTA renders.
- The normal repository gates pass: `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and
  `uv run pytest`.

## Implementation preconditions

1. `RCA-004` is `active` in `governance/registry.yaml`.
2. The implementation slice identifies itself as `LAND1-01` and remains within this specification.
3. Every rendered CTA and legal/trust link resolves to an existing, active-authority-supported
   runtime destination.
4. Marketing wording that presents governed concepts is reconciled against `RRA-009` and
   `RRA-011`; neither authority is redefined or manually duplicated.

Identity, state, document, dependencies, and supersession are authoritative in
`governance/registry.yaml`.
