# RCA-010: Commercial Shell Presentation, Navigation and Filter Implementation Authority

## Outcome

The commercial shell's surfaces at `/app/{language}` and the public legal pages that share its
stylesheets realize the approved Khepri visual language: one navigation per surface with a single
`aria-current`, filters whose applied set stays visible and announced, a responsive posture that
degrades by surface rather than pretending a dense analytical workflow is identical on a phone,
right-to-left rendering built from logical properties, and reproducible accessibility and
visual-regression evidence measured against the approved visual reference pack.

Every destination, capability, figure, and governed word on those surfaces is still `RCA-002`'s,
`RCA-005`'s, `RCA-008`'s, `RCA-009`'s and the calculation families'. This specification adds
presentation and evidence over what already exists, and adds no product capability.

## Why this artifact exists

`docs/product/KHEPRI_UI_UX_MASTER_SPEC.md` merged at `0d3cf87` (`#475`) and the approved visual
reference pack at `e9726b5` (`#476`). The design direction is settled and neither document is
implementation authority; both say so in their own opening lines.

The master specification then names what it cannot license. §18.2 lists `U1-05`, `U1-06` and
`U1-07` as "specification-ready but implementation-blocked", and §18.3 records a fourth gap in
terms: "**No active authority names the shell stylesheet.** `RRA-010:30` excludes `shell.css` and
`shell-components.css` by name; §19 slice 2b is blocked until owner-authored authority covers
them." That gap is real and it is old. `RRA-010` §Scope puts both files "outside this scope" —
"they sit in the journey's asset directory and are the shell's: `shell.html.j2` is their sole
linking template, no `/beta` page loads them, and ownership follows the linking template rather
than the folder". `RRA-012` §Outcome declines them for the same reason and assigns them to `RCA`:
"Those files serve `RCA`'s authenticated commercial surfaces." The roadmap's `C1-07` row records
the same finding from the other side — "`runtime/shell_assets/` is named by no active artifact …
so the first slice that needs a new shell CSS rule needs that scope named first" — and `RCA-008`
claimed `shell_assets/` "for the D1 surfaces only and for no earlier one".

So three active specifications each declined the shell's presentation layer, each for a correct
reason, and nothing received it. This specification is the receiving authority, in the same shape
`RRA-010` received the journey presentation surface `RCA-002` excludes and `RRA-012` received the
component layer `RRA-011` disclaimed.

**The family choice is `RCA` and it is not arbitrary.** The surfaces this specification governs are
the authenticated commercial shell and the public legal pages that share its stylesheets, which
`governance/families/RCA.md` owns. `shell.css` and `shell-components.css` sit in an `RRA` package
directory and are nonetheless `RCA`'s, on the linking-template rule that two active specifications
have already applied to exactly these two files. An `RRA` specification could not govern them:
`RRA-012` refused them by name on the one-family rule, and reversing that reading would contradict
an active specification on the day this one merged.

**Why this is two artifacts and not the single one the master specification §18.4 asked for.**
`governance/templates/specification.md` requires a specification to "include exactly one family in
`depends_on`", and `khepri_gov`'s `_validate_family_links` enforces it. `U1-03`'s files are `RRA`
rendering paths and `U1-05`'s are the commercial shell's, so one artifact could not carry both
without putting a single specification across two families' surfaces — the thing `RRA-012` §Outcome
reads that rule as forbidding. The work is split along the boundary the repository already draws:
active `RRA-015` carries `U1-03` and the `RRA` report surfaces' share of `U1-06` and `U1-07`; this
specification carries `U1-05`, the shell presentation surfaces, and their share of `U1-06` and
`U1-07`. Neither widens the other, and no new governance family is created.

**This specification consumes the design direction; it does not redesign it.** The information
architecture it authorizes is the master specification §D's, its screen contracts §F's, its
component behaviour §G.2's, its right-to-left and bilingual rules §9's, its responsive rules §10's,
its accessibility floors §11's, its state grammar §13's, its asset policy §7's, and its visual
acceptance rules §16.3 and §17's. None of them is reopened here.

## Successor reading

The predecessors this specification succeeds stay `active`. This is clause-level and path-level
successor authority, not whole-artifact retirement, and no predecessor may name `superseded_by` in
the registry while active.

1. **`RRA-010` §Scope, the shell-stylesheet exclusion.** "`src/khepri/rra/journey/assets/shell.css`
   and `shell-components.css` are **outside this scope**." That remains the accurate reading of
   `RRA-010` alone, and this specification is the authority that receives them. `RRA-010` continues
   to govern the `/beta` journey surfaces, `journey.css`, the journey templates and scripts, and
   its three named test modules, entirely and unchanged. The master specification §19 slice 2 —
   the journey half of the `.skip-link` unification — stays `RRA-010`'s; slice 2b, the shell half,
   is authorized here.
2. **`RRA-010` §Exclusions, the cross-surface entry.** Its refusal of "any dependency on a
   presentation value or asset owned by a surface outside this specification's governed scope"
   stays in force for `RRA-010` slices. It is not inverted here: this specification governs the
   shell's own values and does not reach into the journey's.
3. **`RRA-012` §Outcome, the deliberate omission.** "The commercial shell's own component layer —
   `shell.css` and `shell-components.css` … is **not** governed here." Unchanged as a reading of
   `RRA-012`. Whether the two layers should share tokens is a question `RRA-012` expressly left
   open, and this specification does not answer it: `FR-201` keeps each surface's tokens its own.
4. **`RRA-012` §Implementation preconditions item 2 and §Exclusions.** "`U1-03`, `U1-05`, `U1-06`,
   and `U1-07` are **not** authorized here", and the exclusion of global navigation and filter
   bars because "`U1-05` names the first two". `U1-05` and the shell's share of `U1-06` and `U1-07`
   are authorized **here**; `U1-03` and the `RRA` report surfaces' share are `RRA-015`'s. Nothing
   in `RRA-012`'s component layer is reopened.
5. **`RCA-008` §Scope, `src/khepri/runtime/shell_assets/`.** `RCA-008` claims that directory "for
   the D1 surfaces only and for no earlier one". That claim is unchanged for the decision surfaces'
   own rules. This specification succeeds it for **presentation rules shared across shell
   surfaces** — the tokens, layout, navigation, filter, responsive and right-to-left rules the
   approved design language requires — and no slice under this document changes a rule a `D1`
   surface's read model or source map depends on.
6. **`RCA-005` and `RCA-008` §Scope, `shell_api.py` and `shell_templates/`.** Both remain the
   authority for their surfaces' routes, destinations, read models, source maps, and what each
   surface may read. This specification succeeds them for **presentation markup and ARIA state
   only** within `shell_templates/`, and claims no part of `shell_api.py`: no route, no handler, no
   read, no destination tuple, and no capability. A slice that would change what a surface reads or
   where it can go is an `RCA-005` or `RCA-008` slice, not one of these.
7. **`RCA-008` §Exclusions and §Implementation preconditions item 3.** Their statement that the
   chart-grammar, navigation, accessibility-evidence and visual-regression programmes "remain
   `U1-03`, `U1-05`, `U1-06` and `U1-07`'s and need their own authority" is satisfied rather than
   contradicted: this document and `RRA-015` are that authority. `RCA-008` `FR-159` through
   `FR-171` are unchanged, and `FR-170`'s discharge remains `RCA-009` `FR-179`'s.
8. **`RCA-002`.** It remains the authority for the shell's frame, its language rule, its parity
   requirement, and its refusal-of-entry surfaces. This specification changes how that frame is
   presented and evidenced; it changes no frame semantic, no disclosure rule, and no cause-hiding
   guarantee.

## Scope

This specification governs presentation over surfaces that already exist:

- `src/khepri/rra/journey/assets/shell.css` — the shell's token sheet. **Named by no active
  specification** before this one, and the shell's by the linking-template rule `RRA-010` and
  `RRA-012` both apply. The sheet's shipped character is preserved rather than reopened: it stays
  tokens-only, and the test asserting it declares no rules stays passing.
- `src/khepri/rra/journey/assets/shell-components.css` — the shell's component rules. **Named by no
  active specification** before this one.
- `src/khepri/runtime/shell_assets/workspace.css` — shell presentation rules, succeeding
  `RCA-008`'s D1-only claim for shared presentation per §Successor reading item 5.
- `src/khepri/runtime/shell_templates/` — **presentation markup and ARIA state only**: element
  structure, landmark and heading structure, accessible names, `aria-current`, focus targets,
  direction attributes, and class names. No route, handler, destination, read, or capability.
- `src/khepri/runtime/legal_templates/` — the same presentation-only reach, because those pages
  link the shell's stylesheets and would otherwise drift from them.
- `src/khepri/runtime/shell_copy.py` — **the shell's own chrome labels only**, added beside the
  existing entries and carrying the same import-time parity discipline: the text a presentation
  control needs to name itself, such as a filter disclosure, the applied-filter region, and a
  navigation region's accessible name. A chrome label names a region of the interface; the moment a
  word would describe a metric, refusal, caveat, population, version, or governed state, it is
  `RRA-009`'s, `RRA-011`'s, `RRA-014`'s or `RCA-008`'s and belongs there.
- `tests/` — focused navigation, filter, responsive, right-to-left, accessibility and
  visual-regression evidence over the shell surfaces, extending `tests/test_r801_shell_tokens.py`
  and `tests/test_r807_shell_quality.py` and adding modules beside them. No test under another
  specification's Verification is weakened or deleted.

**Not in scope:** `src/khepri/runtime/shell_api.py` and every other `src/khepri/runtime/*.py`
module, the `src/khepri/rca/` packages, the `/beta` journey surfaces and `journey.css`
(`RRA-010`'s), the `RRA` rendering surfaces, charts and report stylesheets (`RRA-006`, `RRA-009`,
`RRA-012`, `RRA-015`'s), the public landing surface and `landing.css`, migrations, object storage,
the composition root, and every route, orchestration and persistence path in the repository.

- **`U1-06` may add accessibility evidence for the commercial shell surfaces**, asserting the
  master specification §11 floors on those surfaces as tests rather than as aspirations.
- **`U1-07` may add visual-regression evidence for named representative shell surfaces**, compared

`U1-03` and the `RRA` report surfaces' share of `U1-06` and `U1-07` are **not** authorized here;

`RRA-010` journey-adoption reading is untouched and undecided by this document. No other `U1` item,
and no other UI programme, is authorized here.

## Requirements

Numbering continues from `RRA-015`, which ends at `FR-192`.

### Navigation and filters

- **FR-193**: The interface never invents capability. A destination enters navigation only in the
  slice that implements it, as `RCA-002` `FR-049` already requires; no "coming soon" entry, no
  disabled control standing in for a future surface, and no result count the governed set does not
  fix. No slice under this specification adds a route, an address, a capability, an authorization
  path, or a product destination.
- **FR-194**: One navigation per surface, with `aria-current="page"` on exactly one entry.
  Duplicated navigation on a surface is a defect. No literal directional glyph serves as a
  navigation affordance, because an arrow does not mirror.
- **FR-195**: The language remains a property of the address under the existing
  language-parameterised prefix, and the switch preserves the reader's surface and position.
  `RCA-002` `FR-047`, `FR-055` and their scenarios are unchanged; this requirement presents them
  and redefines none of them.
- **FR-196**: Every effective filter is visible on the surface that carries the figures it
  qualifies, and the applied set is announced to assistive technology. A filter that narrows a
  figure without being visible on the same screen is a defect. A narrow viewport may collapse the
  filter controls into a disclosure, and **the applied filters stay visible when it does**.
- **FR-197**: Filters remain what `RCA-008` `FR-166` already models them as — the period is a
  source selector, the workspace is the resolved organization scope, and only the view filters
  whose definitions admit them are filters. No slice under this specification adds a filter, sends
  a parameter a view's allowlist does not name, or retains a filter, layout, sort, or dismissal
  state.

### Responsive and right-to-left

- **FR-198**: The responsive posture is the master specification §10's and degrades by surface: a
  table scrolls horizontally inside a focusable region with visible focus rather than collapsing
  into a card list that loses its column relationships; a chart, where one is present, keeps full
  width with reduced label density and is never dropped; a drawer becomes a full-screen sheet with
  focus trapped and restored on close; and trust state stays adjacent to its figure at every width.
  **There is no page-level horizontal overflow at any supported width**, and every scrolling region
  is explicit and bounded.
- **FR-199**: Right-to-left presentation is built from **logical CSS properties only**. The shell's
  stylesheets currently contain no physical directional property and a slice under this document
  does not introduce one. `lang` and `dir` remain server-computed and are never inferred in a
  template; `dir="auto"` continues to isolate customer-controlled mixed-script values; truncation
  is in character units with the full value retained in the DOM so the accessible name stays
  complete; and a caveat, refusal, evidence link, state, or action present in one language is
  present in the other.

### Accessibility and visual evidence on the shell surfaces

- **FR-200**: The master specification §11 floors are asserted per shell surface as tests, in both
  languages, at the supported viewports: a visible focus on every tab stop including scrollable
  regions; focus order following document order with no positive `tabindex`; semantic landmarks
  with meaningful unique accessible names; exactly one `h1` with no skipped heading level; labels
  associated with controls and never a placeholder standing in for one; ARIA only where native
  semantics are insufficient; `role="status"` for refusals and progress; non-color differentiation
  for every trust state; targets of at least 44px on the element a pointer lands on; contrast
  computed rather than asserted; `prefers-reduced-motion` filling a progress track rather than
  freezing it; text scaling to 200% without loss of content or function; and errors announced
  rather than only coloured.
- **FR-201**: A shell presentation value is the shell's. A slice names no custom property, class,
  or asset whose authority sits with the journey or the report surfaces, and introduces no shared
  token layer across the two — the question `RRA-012` left open stays open, and answering it needs
  its own artifact naming both families' paths.
- **FR-202**: A refusal, an empty result, a loading state, and an error are four distinguishable
  screen states, distinguished by structure and wording rather than by decoration. A refusal never
  shares an element or a class with an error, never carries error paint, and keeps the position the
  answer would have occupied. The two governed empty rules stay distinguishable, exactly as
  `RCA-008` `FR-163` requires.
- **FR-203**: Motion explains change and does not advertise the interface. No bounce, elastic
  easing, parallax, decorative floating element, constant motion, or animated number counting; a
  positional transition on a drawer or dialog is short; and no transition is load-bearing under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **FR-204**: Visual-regression evidence compares named representative rendered shell surfaces
  against approved design evidence on **hierarchy, density, spacing rhythm, typography, visual
  states, RTL, responsive behaviour, and asset fidelity**. Pixel-identical equality is **not**
  required where responsive layout legitimately adapts; visual-language drift is a defect even
  where every other test passes. The evidence is deterministic and reproducible from the
  repository, and uses the browser automation the repository already ships rather than a new
  visual-testing platform, service, or hosted baseline store.
- **FR-205**: Visual evidence is evidence, not truth. No baseline, screenshot, or reference image
  is read as a source of a figure, a route, a capability, a refusal reason, or a governed word, and
  no slice copies a value out of one. Where an image and an active specification disagree, the
  specification wins.

### Assets

- **FR-206**: The master specification §7 asset policy is binding and unrelaxed. No CSS-drawn
  illustration, div-art, CSS pyramid, CSS scarab, pseudo-element used as artwork, emoji icon,
  Unicode glyph standing in for an icon, or one-off inline SVG illustration authored by an
  implementer. No external font, image, style, script, or CDN dependency, which the shipped
  `default-src 'none'` CSP forbids in any case, and no inline script or style that would weaken it.
  An icon or typeface enters only through the existing licence-plus-audited-digest process, which
  this specification does not amend and does not bypass. **Data-driven charts are the one expected
  programmatic drawing**, and they are `RRA-015`'s rather than this document's. Where an approved
  asset is absent, the surface ships without it rather than with an approximation.

## Exclusions

This specification authorizes none of the following, and a slice claiming it does is outside its
specification within the meaning of Constitution IV:

- Any new route, address, product destination, navigation entry for an unimplemented surface,
  capability, authorization path, entitlement, or "coming soon" surface.
- Any calculation, figure, metric, population, formula, reason code, caveat code, semantic view,
  view version, or read-model change. A surface that computes remains the defect `RCA-008` `FR-159`
  names, in a template and a stylesheet as much as in Python.
- Any change to `src/khepri/runtime/shell_api.py`, the `src/khepri/rca/` packages, the composition
  root, the semantic-query orchestration, or any other runtime module.
- Any new customer-visible word describing a metric, refusal, caveat, population, version, or
  governed state. Those are `RRA-009`'s, `RRA-011`'s, `RRA-014`'s and `RCA-008`'s. The shell's own
  chrome labels are the sole exception, bounded by §Scope.
- The `/beta` journey surfaces, `journey.css`, the journey templates and scripts, the pending
  `RRA-010` journey-adoption reading, and the public landing surface and its stylesheet.
- The `RRA` report and evidence render surfaces, the chart grammar, `report.css` and
  `report.print.css`. Those are `RRA-006`'s, `RRA-012`'s and `RRA-015`'s.
- Any shared token layer across the shell and the journey or report surfaces.
- Any persistence, schema, migration, object-store change, cache, pre-aggregation, materialized
  view, sampling, or retained layout, filter, sort, dismissal, or preference state.
- Any telemetry event, product-analytics event, counter, access record, audit record, or
  content-bearing log, of any kind, content-free or not. `KHEPRI-DEC-015` §3 stands unamended and
  `R8-08`, `T1-07`, `D1-11` and `W1-11` remain unauthorized.
- Any change to privacy, retention, provenance, evidence derivation, semantic admission,
  organization isolation, authorization, cross-organization boundaries, refusal semantics, or
  product capability exposure.
- Any billing, agency tenancy, connector, scheduled delivery, public API, export, sharing, or
  natural-language surface.
- Any new package, dependency, CI workflow, or runtime behaviour, and any weakening of the shipped
  Content Security Policy.
- A general accessibility or visual-regression programme over surfaces this specification does not
  govern, and any surface-inventory guard beyond the surfaces in §Scope.

## Higher-order invariants

Presentation may change. Truth may not.

- No governed calculation, metric definition, semantic admission rule, evidence derivation,
  provenance record, privacy boundary, retention horizon, organization isolation boundary,
  authorization decision, refusal semantic, cross-organization boundary, or exposed product
  capability is altered by any slice under this document, and the `M4` behaviour already accepted
  is unchanged.
- Where this document and an active specification disagree on what a figure means, who may see it,
  or what is retained, the active specification wins.
- A refusal is a governed result the reader is entitled to, never an error to be hidden or styled
  into something else.
- No surface traps a reader: every page, refusal surfaces included, carries at least one exit.
- The approved visual reference pack is acceptance evidence for the surfaces it covers and silent
  on the rest; it carries no authority of its own and supplies no product fact.

## Retention, privacy, and telemetry

Unchanged from `RCA-002`, `RCA-005`, `RCA-008` and `KHEPRI-DEC-033`. The surfaces retain nothing of
their own: no layout, filter, sort, or dismissal state, and no record that a figure was viewed.
Content shown is the run's, and its retention is the run's. `KHEPRI-DEC-015` §3's prohibition on
repeat-use telemetry is not amended here.

## Verification

Every slice under this specification proves, with focused tests:

- Exactly one navigation per surface with exactly one `aria-current="page"`, and no navigation
  entry for a surface the shell does not serve — asserted against the shell's own destinations
  rather than against a list a test happens to remember.
- No route, handler, destination, or capability changed, by static scope evidence over the
  authorized paths.
- Every effective filter is visible on the surface carrying the figures it qualifies, the applied
  set is announced, and a narrow viewport that collapses the controls keeps the applied filters
  visible.
- No page-level horizontal overflow at any supported width in either language; a wide table scrolls
  inside a focusable region with visible focus; a drawer traps focus and restores it on close.
- The `FR-200` accessibility floors hold per shell surface in both languages at the supported
  viewports, measured in the real browser the repository already drives, with contrast computed
  rather than asserted.
- No stylesheet rule this specification adds introduces a physical directional property, and
  `shell.css` remains tokens-only.
- A refusal, an empty result, a loading state and an error render distinguishably, and no refusal
  shares an element or a class with an error.
- `FR-204`'s visual evidence is reproducible from the repository and deterministic across two runs,
  and no baseline, screenshot, or reference image is read as a source of a figure, route,
  capability, refusal reason, or governed word.
- Every chrome label this specification adds exists in both languages under the same import-time
  parity discipline the surrounding entries use.
- No new telemetry, audit record, counter, access record, content-bearing log, or retained
  presentation state is emitted, and the shipped Content Security Policy is unweakened.

The normal repository gates pass: `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and
`uv run pytest`. Every new file scores 10.00 in the required server-side CodeScene Code Health
Review and no tracked hotspot declines. That is the standing gate on implementation work under this
document, not a record of any one pull request's result.

## Implementation preconditions

1. `RCA-010` is `active` in `governance/registry.yaml`, and `RCA-002`, `RCA-005` and `RCA-008`
   remain `active`.
2. An implementation slice identifies itself as `U1-05`, as the shell's share of `U1-06` or
   `U1-07`, or as the shell presentation work the master specification §19 names, and stays within
   §Scope. `U1-03` and the `RRA` report surfaces' share of `U1-06` and `U1-07` are **not**
   authorized here and belong to `RRA-015`. `U1-01`, `U1-02` and `U1-04` are delivered under
   `RRA-012` and `RRA-013` and are not reopened.
3. A slice's only edit inside `shell_templates/` and `legal_templates/` is presentation markup and
   ARIA state. A change to what a surface reads, where it can go, or what it may do is an
   `RCA-005`, `RCA-008` or `RCA-009` slice and is made there.
4. A slice's only addition to `shell_copy.py` is a shell chrome label, carrying the import-time
   parity discipline the surrounding entries use. A missing or wrong governed word is a defect
   against the authority that owns it and is fixed there.
5. A slice's edit to `workspace.css` is a shared shell presentation rule. A rule a `D1` surface's
   read model or source map depends on is `RCA-008`'s and is changed under `RCA-008`.
6. Slices are small and independently verifiable, as Constitution IV requires. A slice does not
   widen this specification, its privacy boundary, its runtime boundary, or its data use.

Identity, state, document, dependencies, and supersession are authoritative in
`governance/registry.yaml`.
