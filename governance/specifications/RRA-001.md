# RRA-001: Beta Invitations and Isolated Sessions

## Outcome

An invited beta participant can establish one isolated, pseudonymous session and give explicit
consent before any customer content is accepted.

## Requirements

- Issue high-entropy, single-use invitation secrets and persist only a strong salted hash.
- Reject expired, redeemed, malformed, or replayed invitations without revealing which check
  failed.
- Redeeming a valid invitation creates an opaque owner ID and opaque session ID that contain no
  email address, profile identifier, or business identity.
- Bind every upload, fact package, narrative, export, job, and deletion operation to the opaque
  owner and session; fail closed on cross-session access.
- Record explicit consent version and timestamp before enabling upload.
- Set the content expiry instant to seven days after session creation.
- Do not create passwords, profiles, billing identities, persistent workspaces, or a public
  signup path.
- Preserve the opaque owner ID as the only future attachment point for separately approved
  commercial authentication.

## Exclusions

Email delivery mechanics, commercial identity, account recovery, organization membership, and
long-lived customer workspaces are not authorized.

## Verification

Tests cover single use, hashed secrets, expiry, consent-before-upload, identifier opacity,
replay resistance, and cross-session isolation.

This specification is draft and does not authorize product implementation.
