# RRA-013: Catalog Evidence Supply to the Report Surfaces

## Outcome

Every rendering surface receives, for each figure it states, the evidence `RRA-011`'s catalog
already serves about that figure — its governed version, unit kind, precision, inputs, and the
package's coverage identity — **from the bundle it already renders**, so that `RRA-012`'s evidence
drawer can be placed beside each figure and the catalog's citation route and the report surfaces
read one projection rather than two.

**This specification supplies data. It adds no figure, no word, no component, and no surface.** The
drawer's structure, labels, and behaviour are `RRA-012`'s (FR-096 to FR-098) and are merged; what a
metric means is `RRA-011`'s; what a refusal says is `RRA-009`'s. This governs the path by which
existing evidence reaches the renderer, and nothing else.

**Why this authority is needed, stated plainly.** `RRA-012`'s Scope note 2 deferred the drawer's
supply *"to its own slice under its own authority"*, and its Verification requires a drawer driven by
a derived-family figure whose `inputs` the catalog returns as `None`. `U1-04`'s structure merged with
that drawer **placed on no page** and a guard test holding the line, because an unsupplied drawer
would render every field unavailable for a reason FR-096a does not mean. No active specification
governs how that data reaches `build_context`: `RRA-012` claims only `_CHROME`'s label binding in
`html.py` and excludes *"its figure construction, cell building, and audit projection"* from itself;
`RRA-011` governs the catalog's data and *"deliberately"* does not name the consuming surfaces
(`RRA-011`:69-73); no specification names `bundle.py` at all. This is the receiving authority for that
path, in the same shape `RRA-012` received the component layer and `RRA-011` the catalog.

**The family is `RRA`.** Every path named below is under `src/khepri/rra/`, and
`governance/families/RRA.md` owns "consistent report surfaces". No other family's surface is touched.

## The mechanism, traced rather than assumed

`RRA-012`'s Scope note 2 described the supply as a **materialization-path change**: a three-implementer
`render_materialized` contract, three `build_context` callers, `pipeline.py`, both wirings, and an
Excel renderer that displays no HTML component. **That enumeration was accurate about the call graph
and wrong about the shape of the change**, because it assumed the projection must be *threaded
through* the renderers. It need not be. Three facts about the tree, checked at `b9aeacd`:

1. **Every renderer already receives one object — the `ReportBundle`.** `MaterializedRenderer`
   (`report_artifacts.py:143`) takes a bundle and nothing else; `pipeline.py:270` calls it uniformly;
   `pdf.py:213` and `html.py:353` build their context from the bundle; `report_api.py:1004` builds the
   audit projection from one. A field **on the bundle** reaches all of them with no signature, no
   Protocol, no pipeline, and no wiring change. Excel ignores it exactly as it ignores `narrative`.
2. **The bundle is assembled from the package that holds the evidence.** `ReportBundle.of(package)`
   (`bundle.py:893`) has the `FactPackage` in hand: `package.facts`, `package.series`,
   `package.comparisons` — the same three collections `report_api._stored_fact` reads — and the
   package carries `coverage_manifest_identity` and `coverage_signatures`. **The three record types
   do not carry the same fields, and the evidence states exactly what each carries.** A `Fact`
   (`facts.py:249`) has `precision`, `inputs`, and its own `formula_version`. A `FactSeries`
   (`facts.py:284`) and a `FactComparison` (`facts.py:310`) have `precision` and their own
   `formula_version` and **no `inputs` field at all** — `_cited_figure` already answers `None` for
   them via `hasattr`, and this specification makes that a governed absence rather than an accident
   of attribute lookup. Assembling evidence there is the same act as assembling `figures` there.
3. **A derived figure's evidence is absent by construction, not by lookup failure.** The `RRA-008`
   analysis facts are computed during assembly and retained by nothing (`_stored_fact`'s docstring,
   `report_api.py:822`). Their `precision` and `inputs` are `None` because no retained record states
   them, and `RRA-011`:204 forbids re-deriving them to fill the gap. The bundle records that absence
   as the catalog does — `None`, not an empty list — which is what FR-096a's unavailable state renders.
   **A derived figure's formula version is not the package's.** `package.formula_version` is the
   `RRA-004` package formula; a comparison, growth, basket, or concentration figure was produced under
   its family's own version, which the bundle already holds: the `_FAMILIES` table (`bundle.py:1567`)
   that derives each section carries `version=lambda: <family>.<FAMILY>_FORMULA_VERSION`. That is the
   same registry `definitions._METRIC_VERSIONS` (`definitions.py:129`) is built from, so the bundle
   and the catalog route report one version for one figure without the bundle importing the catalog
   — which it could not, since `definitions` imports `wording` and `wording` imports `bundle`.

**Consequence: the supply is a bundle field plus one context key, not a renderer contract change.**
`pipeline.py`, `report_artifacts.py`, `excel.py`, and both wirings are **not in Scope** and are not
edited. `RRA-012`'s Scope note is not amended: it correctly deferred the work; its enumeration of the
path is superseded by this trace, and this document records that rather than editing history.

**What the bundle carries is language-neutral.** Definition text is per language and is `RRA-011`'s
vocabulary (`wording.METRIC_DESCRIPTIONS`). It does not go on the bundle; `build_context` binds it into
`chrome` per language, exactly as `sections`, `chart_descriptions` and `labels` are bound today
(`html.py:176-178`), so a template resolves a definition the way it resolves a section heading.

**What the bundle identity must and must not absorb.** `bundle_id` is a digest over
`as_document()`, and delivery and reconciliation rely on it as a content address. The evidence
splits in two against that guarantee:

- **Per-citation evidence is derived from records the identity already covers** — the cited fact's
  `precision`, `inputs`, and `formula_version` are functions of the figure, the package version, and
  the mapping version, all of which `as_document()` already digests. It is **excluded** from the
  document: including it would rename every report for no change in what was published.
- **Coverage is not.** `BundleIdentity.as_document()` (`bundle.py:560`) carries neither
  `coverage_manifest_identity` nor `coverage_signatures`. Two packages built from the same upload and
  profile under different compatible coverage manifests can therefore state identical figures while
  the drawer publishes different coverage evidence — and today they would share one `bundle_id` and
  one report reference. That breaks the content address the moment coverage becomes visible. So
  **coverage enters `BundleIdentity`** — the manifest identity and the signature identities, which
  are provenance in exactly the sense that module's docstring gives the word — and
  **`BUNDLE_VERSION` advances** (`rra006.bundle.v7` → `v8`), because the identity document's shape
  changed and a consumer that selected its reader from `v7` must not find an extra field. FR-105
  makes both halves testable.

## Scope

- `src/khepri/rra/bundle.py` — a `CitedEvidence` record and an `evidence` field on `ReportBundle`,
  assembled in `ReportBundle.of` from the package's retained records and coverage, one entry per
  distinct `citation_id` the bundle's figures cite. Package-level coverage is carried once, not per
  figure (see FR-104); coverage also enters `BundleIdentity` and `BUNDLE_VERSION` advances (FR-105).
  Nothing else in this module: no figure, section, caveat, or reason behaviour changes. No active
  specification names this file; `RRA-006` defines the `ReportBundle` *concept* — one immutable
  package version, narrative version, source digest, mapping version — and binding coverage
  provenance into that identity is that concept, applied to a field it did not yet name.
- `src/khepri/rra/rendering/html.py` — **two points, named exactly.** (a) `build_context`'s audit
  projection (`_audit_region`) carries `bundle.evidence` under one key, `evidence`, keyed by
  `citation_id`, with the definition text resolved per language beside it, **and the bundle's
  coverage under a second key, `coverage`, once** — never copied into the per-citation entries
  (FR-104). The merged drawer macro reads coverage from its `given` mapping today; the `RRA-012`
  placement slice passes the context's `coverage` to the drawer as its own argument, adjusting the
  macro's signature under its own authority, rather than merging it into each entry. (b) `_CHROME` binds
  `METRIC_DESCRIPTIONS[language]` under `metric_descriptions`, beside the `RRA-011` and `RRA-009`
  vocabulary already bound there. `RRA-012` claims `_CHROME`'s FR-095a labels and no other behaviour
  of this module for itself; it does not forbid another authority, exactly as `RRA-010`'s exclusion
  of `html.py` did not forbid `RRA-012`. Figure construction and cell building stay unchanged.
- `src/khepri/rra/rendering/pdf.py` — **one context key**, `evidence_open`, set true by the print
  surface only. A closed `<details>` prints collapsed, so the printed appendix would carry the
  drawer's opener and none of its evidence; the flag lets `_evidence.html.j2` render the drawer
  `open` in print. Font handling, print stylesheet, and every other behaviour of this module are
  unchanged. `RRA-012` names this file only to exclude it from itself.
- `tests/` — the supply's tests, and the identity-stability test FR-105 requires.

**Two things this Scope does not include, and why.**

**1. Placing the drawer in `_evidence.html.j2` is `RRA-012`'s work, not this document's.** FR-096
already requires the drawer *"beside that figure"*, and `_evidence.html.j2` is in `RRA-012`'s Scope.
Once this specification's supply is merged, a `U1-04` slice under `RRA-012` places the drawer in the
evidence figures table, passes the print flag, and **deletes**
`test_no_surface_places_the_drawer_before_its_supply_exists` — the one PR entitled to. Two active
specifications over one template path would fail closed under `AGENTS.md`, so this document does not
name it.

**2. Making the catalog's citation route read the bundle's evidence is an `RRA-011` slice.**
`RRA-011`:169-170 requires a catalog route to *"read the projection the report surfaces already render
from, never assemble a second one"*. Today `report_api._cited_figure` assembles that projection
itself. After this supply exists, the route should read `bundle.evidence` and `_cited_figure` and
`_stored_fact` should go; that is a change to `report_api.py`, which `RRA-011` governs, and it is
recorded here as the follow-on that closes the duplicate rather than claimed.

## Requirements

Numbering continues from `RRA-012`, which ends at `FR-101`.

- **FR-102**: `ReportBundle` MUST carry, for every distinct `citation_id` among its figures, one
  evidence record holding the citation identifier, the metric code, the unit kind, the governed
  formula version, `precision`, and `inputs`, under these rules and no others:
  - a retained `Fact` supplies its own `precision`, `inputs`, and `formula_version`;
  - a retained `FactSeries` or `FactComparison` supplies its own `precision` and `formula_version`,
    and `inputs` is `None` — the record type has no such field, and none MAY be invented for it;
  - a derived analysis figure retained by no record has `precision` and `inputs` `None`, and its
    formula version is the assembling family's own version from the bundle's family table — never
    `package.formula_version`, which names the package formula and not the family's.
  `None` means *no retained record states this* in every case, and a consumer MUST NOT distinguish
  the two absent cases. This restates `RRA-011`:204 at the point where re-derivation would be easiest.
- **FR-103**: The evidence MUST be assembled from exactly these sources: the package's retained
  records — `facts`, `series`, `comparisons` — for a stored figure's precision, inputs, and formula
  version; the bundle's own family table for a derived figure's formula version; and the package's
  coverage fields for coverage. No evidence value MAY be computed, and none MAY be read from a
  rendered surface, a store, a route, or the catalog module.
- **FR-104**: The package's coverage — `coverage_manifest_identity` and `coverage_signatures` — MUST be
  carried once at bundle level and never attributed to an individual figure. A `Fact` carries no
  population identifier (`RRA-011`), and FR-097 forbids presenting one per figure; the supply MUST
  NOT create the relationship the drawer is forbidden to display.
- **FR-105**: The bundle identity MUST absorb coverage and nothing else from this specification.
  `coverage_manifest_identity` and the coverage signature identities enter `BundleIdentity` and its
  `as_document()`, and `BUNDLE_VERSION` advances once. Per-citation evidence MUST NOT enter
  `as_document()`. Tests MUST prove: two packages identical but for their coverage manifest yield
  different `bundle_id`s; an identical package yields an identical `bundle_id` across two assemblies;
  and the `bundle_version` provenance row reads the advanced version.
- **FR-106**: `build_context` MUST carry the evidence into the audit projection under two keys:
  `evidence`, keyed by `citation_id`, with each entry's definition text resolved in the document's
  language from `RRA-011`'s vocabulary; and `coverage`, once, at bundle level. No per-citation entry
  MAY carry coverage (FR-104). It MUST NOT carry a fact's `value`, and MUST NOT carry any
  Internal-tier field (`presentation-visibility-matrix.md` §A.6). The business context MUST NOT gain
  either key: citation identifiers, versions, and coverage are tier A (§A.5).
- **FR-107**: The print surface MUST set `evidence_open` so a drawer rendered in the printed appendix
  is open; the web evidence page MUST NOT set it. A collapsed drawer on paper is evidence the reader
  was told exists and cannot see.
- **FR-108**: The supply MUST reach every renderer through the bundle alone. `MaterializedRenderer`,
  `render_materialized`, `pipeline.py`, and the renderer wiring MUST NOT change; a slice that widens
  the renderer contract to carry evidence is outside this specification.

## Exclusions

This specification authorizes none of the following, and a slice claiming it does is outside its
specification within the meaning of Constitution IV:

- Any new metric, population, formula, reason code, caveat code, or figure.
- Any calculation, re-derivation, re-rounding, or re-formatting of a published figure — including
  re-deriving a derived fact's inputs or precision to fill an absent field.
- Any new customer-visible word. The definition text is `RRA-011`'s; the drawer's labels are
  `RRA-012`'s; nothing here authors either.
- Any change to a template, stylesheet, or component. Placement of the drawer is `RRA-012`'s.
- Any change to `report_api.py` or to what the catalog routes serve. The route's adoption of the
  bundle's evidence is an `RRA-011` slice.
- Any change to the renderer contract, `pipeline.py`, `excel.py`, or the wirings (FR-108).
- Retention of a `ReportBundle`, its evidence, or any lineage record. `KHEPRI-DEC-032` records that
  retention is not required; this does not require it either. Source-to-surface lineage is `T1-06`'s.
- **Any new telemetry event, of any kind.** Active `KHEPRI-DEC-015`, and `RRA-010`, `RCA-003`,
  `RRA-011`, and `RRA-012` each exclude new telemetry events under their own authority.
- Journey adoption (`RRA-010`:88) and the commercial shell (`RCA`).

## Verification

- A test proves every distinct citation among a bundle's figures has exactly one evidence record;
  that a retained `Fact`'s record carries its `precision`, `inputs`, and `formula_version`; that a
  retained series or comparison record carries its `precision` and `formula_version` with `inputs`
  `None`; and that a derived fact's record carries `None` for both with its family's version —
  driven by a package whose comparison section publishes, so all three shapes are present.
- A test proves no evidence value equals a fact's `value` or rendered text (FR-103, FR-106).
- A test proves coverage appears once at bundle level and on no per-citation record (FR-104).
- Tests prove FR-105's three claims: coverage-only differences change `bundle_id`, identical packages
  do not, and the `bundle_version` row reads the advanced version.
- A test proves the business context carries neither `evidence` nor `coverage`, and the audit context
  carries `evidence` keyed by `citation_id` and `coverage` once, in both languages, with Arabic
  definition text in the Arabic context.
- A test proves the print context sets `evidence_open` and the web contexts do not (FR-107).
- A test proves `MaterializedRenderer.render_materialized`'s signature and `pipeline.py`'s call are
  unchanged (FR-108), by reading them rather than by asserting a constant.
- The normal repository gates pass: `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and
  `uv run pytest`.

1. `RRA-013` is `active` in `governance/registry.yaml`.
2. The implementation slice identifies itself as the `U1-04` supply slice and remains within this
   specification. Drawer placement (`RRA-012`) and the route's adoption (`RRA-011`) follow it as
   separate slices under their own authorities.

4. A slice's only edits to `html.py` are the two points named in Scope; its only edit to `pdf.py` is
   the one context key; its only edits to `bundle.py` are the evidence record, field, and assembly,
   the coverage fields on `BundleIdentity`, and the `BUNDLE_VERSION` advance.

Identity, state, document, dependencies, and supersession are authoritative in
`governance/registry.yaml`.
