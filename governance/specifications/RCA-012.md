# RCA-012 — The shell serves its own hero artwork

**Status:** PROPOSED. This document governs nothing until the sole owner merges it to `main`
(`governance/CONSTITUTION.md` §II). It was drafted by automation, which "checks facts and
consistency; it does not approve changes or act as a separate authority" (§I).

**Family:** `RCA`.
**Depends on:** `RCA-010`.

---

## Why this document exists

The owner's approved Product UI handoff, imported at `#512`
(`docs/ui/design_handoff_khepri_product_ui/`), places supplied hero artwork on the two `/app`
workspace surfaces the next slice builds — its screens 01 Home and 05 Insights. Its §6 states the
placement, the crop, the scrim and the loading behaviour, and requires the artwork be shipped as
compressed derivatives rather than as the supplied 2.4 MB PNG.

**The artwork cannot be served today, and a slice that styles the shell around it would ship a
surface whose image 404s.** Two independent reasons, both verified at `124a6f1`:

| Fact | Verified at `124a6f1` |
|---|---|
| Where the supplied source lives | `docs/ui/design_handoff_khepri_product_ui/assets/khepri-hero.png`, SHA-256 `12a68450…a3afd6` |
| Do any derivatives exist under `src/`? | **No.** `git ls-files` finds no `khepri-hero.jpg`, `.webp` or `@2x` variant anywhere in the package tree |
| What the shell serves | five assets, by exact name — three stylesheets and two typefaces (`shell_api.py`'s `_ASSETS`) |
| May a slice add to that allowlist? | **Not under `RCA-010`.** Its §Scope places `src/khepri/runtime/shell_api.py` **and every other `src/khepri/runtime/*.py` module** under *Not in scope* |
| Does the deployed image mount the journey's asset route? | **No.** `wiring.py:580-592` mounts commercial, external-auth, legal, landing and shell — not journey |

So the allowlist refuses the file, and the file does not exist. The handoff asserts that allowlist
additions are "the only permitted edits" to `shell_api.py`, but a `docs/` file confers no authority
(Constitution Article IV), and the imported package's own authority notice records that conflict.
This document is the artifact that resolves it.

**`RCA-010` is not amended.** Its refusal of `src/khepri/runtime/*.py` stands for every other
module and every other purpose. This document carves out the asset allowlist alone, exactly as
`RCA-011` did for the shell's typefaces, and is deliberately the same shape.

---

## Outcome

The shell serves its own hero artwork from its own address, as compressed derivatives carrying an
audited digest, so a `/app` presentation slice can place the approved artwork against an asset that
actually resolves in the shipped image.

## Requirements

- **FR-212**: The shell serves the hero artwork from its own address. The derivatives ship as
  package data under `src/khepri/rra/journey/assets/` and are served by exact name through the
  shell's existing allowlist mechanism, not by directory listing, so a file added to that package
  is not served by arriving.
- **FR-213**: Every shipped derivative carries an audited SHA-256 digest, verified at load, in the
  same discipline `rendering/fonts.py` applies to the typefaces. A binary whose bytes drift from
  the manifest is **refused at load rather than quietly served**. The supplied source PNG's digest
  is recorded as provenance: `12a68450bf6edf1b7aec67a0cdae1741afae5fa81f4fdf6ef7a6a28266a3afd6`.
- **FR-214**: The derivatives are generated from that exact supplied source and from nothing else.
  The generating step, its parameters and the resulting digests are recorded in the implementing
  slice, so "the approved artwork" and "the bytes in the tree" are the same thing and something
  checks that they are.
- **FR-215**: The supplied 2.4 MB PNG is **not** served. It remains design material under `docs/`;
  only the compressed derivatives reach a customer surface, which is what the handoff §6 requires.
- **FR-216**: No external reference is introduced. No CDN, no image host, no runtime download, no
  `@import`. The shipped `default-src 'none'` CSP is unweakened, and the artwork resolves to the
  shell's own asset path.
- **FR-217**: A shell stylesheet names no journey address. `RCA-011` `FR-208` and `RCA-010`
  `FR-201` are unchanged and unrelaxed: this document moves the *asset* into the shell's ownership
  rather than permitting the shell to reach into the journey's.
- **FR-218**: The artwork is decorative and carries no governed meaning. It states no figure, no
  population, no refusal, no caveat and no state. Its alternative text is supplied through the
  existing bilingual copy mechanism rather than hardcoded in a template, and a surface that fails
  to load it remains correct, legible and complete.

## Exclusions

**Not in scope:** every other `src/khepri/runtime/*.py` module and every non-allowlist line of
`shell_api.py` — no route, no handler, no destination, no capability, no authorization path, no
read, and no change to any existing entry; `src/khepri/rra/journey/routes.py` and the `/beta`
journey surfaces, which are `RRA-010`'s and whose half of this artwork is a separate RRA-family
artifact not drafted here (`governance/templates/specification.md` admits exactly one family in
`depends_on`, and `khepri_gov` enforces it); the `RRA` rendering surfaces, charts and report
stylesheets; the landing surface and `landing.css`; the composition root; migrations; object
storage; and every route, orchestration and persistence path in the repository.

**No existing `_ASSETS` entry is altered or removed**, and no journey address is named. This
document authorizes no layout, no component, no typography and no color change — the placement
rules the handoff §6 states are realized only under a presentation slice governed by `RCA-010`,
whose §Scope already admits the shell's stylesheets.

## Verification

- The derivatives are served from the shell's own address by exact name, proven over a real HTTP
  origin rather than asserted from the allowlist dict — the discipline
  `tests/test_rca011_shell_font_load.py` established for the typefaces, because an allowlist entry
  naming a file that the shipped image does not carry passes every in-process test and 404s in
  production.
- A file dropped into the assets package without an allowlist entry is **not** served.
- A derivative whose bytes drift from the audited digest is refused at load.
- The supplied 2.4 MB PNG is not reachable from any customer surface.
- No existing `_ASSETS` entry changed: the three stylesheets and two typefaces are served exactly
  as before.
- No external host appears in any shipped stylesheet or template, and the CSP is unweakened.
- The alternative text is present in both languages through the existing copy mechanism, and a
  surface rendered with the artwork absent remains legible and complete.
