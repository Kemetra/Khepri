# RCA-003: Public Legal and Trust Surfaces

## Outcome

Khepri may expose a small bilingual set of public, non-authenticated legal and trust pages that
accurately describe the service without widening identity, billing, retention, hosting, or data-use
authority.

These pages are informational surfaces only. They do not create new customer rights, payment
mechanics, account capabilities, or data-processing behavior beyond what active governance and a
signed customer agreement already permit.

## Requirements

Numbering continues from `RCA-002`, which ends at `FR-061`.

### Surface inventory

- **FR-062**: The public legal/trust surface set MUST be closed and limited to: Privacy Policy,
  Data Protection, Terms and Conditions, Contact Us, About Us, and a Refund & Void status page.
- **FR-063**: Every public legal/trust surface MUST be addressable in Arabic and English with
  equivalent meaning, correct language metadata, and RTL layout for Arabic.
- **FR-064**: The public legal/trust surfaces MUST NOT require authentication and MUST NOT reveal
  organization, account, membership, dataset, analysis, report, or other customer-specific state.
- **FR-065**: Unknown public legal/trust paths MUST return the ordinary public not-found behavior;
  they MUST NOT reuse an authenticated non-disclosure surface or imply that a protected object
  exists.

### Accuracy and no invented authority

- **FR-066**: Legal copy MUST distinguish current fact from future intent. A production hosting
  region, certification, subprocessor list, retention period, SLA, recovery guarantee, or security
  control MUST NOT be stated as current unless it is already true for the environment being
  described.
- **FR-067**: Khepri MUST NOT claim ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR compliance, Egyptian PDPL
  compliance, or any other certification/compliance status merely because the product is designed
  with related controls. Where a law may apply, the page may describe it as an applicable legal
  framework without claiming legal certification.
- **FR-068**: The Privacy Policy MUST describe, at minimum, the operator identity, privacy contact,
  categories of data processed, purposes, processor/controller role distinction where applicable,
  service-provider/subprocessor use, international-transfer position, retention approach, security
  summary, and data-subject request path. Any unresolved field MUST block publication rather than
  ship as a placeholder.
- **FR-069**: The Data Protection page MUST describe only implemented or explicitly environment-
  scoped safeguards. It MUST NOT turn roadmap intentions into present-tense guarantees.
- **FR-070**: Terms and Conditions MUST identify the contracting/operator entity, acceptable-use
  boundaries, customer responsibility for lawful data use, analytical-result limitations, beta
  status where applicable, intellectual-property allocation, suspension/termination principles,
  liability/contract-reference approach, governing law, dispute forum/process, and contact route.
  Any unresolved legally operative field MUST block publication.
- **FR-071**: Contact Us MUST publish only verified contact channels and the current legal/operator
  identity. No fabricated address, telephone number, or role mailbox may be introduced.
- **FR-072**: About Us MUST describe Khepri as a governed retail decision platform and MUST NOT
  narrow the product to pharmacy-only positioning.

### Refund and billing boundary

- **FR-073**: Until public paid self-service billing is active under its own authority, the Refund &
  Void surface MUST state only that no general public self-service refund policy currently applies
  and that private commercial fees are governed by the applicable agreement and law.
- **FR-074**: This specification MUST NOT introduce prices, subscriptions, invoices, payment
  providers, refund windows, credits, void mechanics, chargebacks, or billing entitlements. Those
  require successor commercial/billing authority.

### Privacy and data-use boundary

- **FR-075**: These pages MUST NOT expand Khepri's collection, retention, analytics, telemetry,
  training, advertising, model-provider, or sharing behavior. Legal copy describes authorized
  behavior; it does not authorize new behavior.
- **FR-076**: Customer-uploaded operational data MUST be described as customer-controlled data
  processed to provide the service, subject to the applicable agreement and active data-use
  authority. The public copy MUST NOT claim ownership of customer business data merely because it
  is uploaded.
- **FR-077**: The pages MUST NOT promise a deletion or export workflow that does not yet exist.
  Where a right may apply, the copy MUST provide a request route and distinguish the legal request
  process from product self-service functionality.

### Presentation and placement

- **FR-078**: Legal/trust links MAY appear in a public footer or other non-authenticated navigation
  only when the destination surface is implemented. No dead legal link may render.
- **FR-079**: Adding these links to an authenticated shell footer MUST NOT alter shell
  authorization, active-organization resolution, or the closed authenticated surface inventory
  governed by `RCA-002`; they are external informational destinations, not organization-scoped
  shell surfaces.
- **FR-080**: Public legal/trust pages MUST carry response security headers and MUST not introduce
  inline scripts, inline styles, third-party trackers, analytics cookies, or consent-management
  behavior under this specification.

## Required publication inputs

Implementation may be prepared, but public publication of Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, and
Contact Us is blocked until the owner supplies and verifies:

1. legal/operator entity name;
2. legal or registered business address if one is to be published or legally required;
3. privacy contact route;
4. support/general contact route;
5. security contact route, if presented;
6. governing law and dispute forum/process for the public Terms;
7. effective date.

A single verified mailbox may satisfy several contact roles. No requirement here forces separate
addresses.

## Exclusions

This specification does not authorize:

- billing, subscriptions, payment-provider integration, invoices, credits, or chargebacks;
- a commercial refund window or cancellation entitlement;
- new telemetry, cookies, advertising, customer-content training, or model-provider data use;
- new retention, export, deletion, legal-hold, or workspace behavior;
- infrastructure, deployment, hosting-region, backup, or disaster-recovery changes;
- certification claims or legal conclusions;
- public signup, authentication, account recovery, or organization-management changes;
- changes to retail calculations, report contracts, or `RRA` analytical behavior.

## Implementation slices

Once this specification is active on `main`, implementation SHOULD proceed as small slices:

1. **LEGAL1-01 — Public legal route/content framework**: bilingual static render path, shared
   security headers, no customer context, closed route inventory, placeholder-publication guard.
2. **LEGAL1-02 — Privacy + Data Protection**: publish only after required operator/contact fields
   are verified.
3. **LEGAL1-03 — Terms + Contact + About**: publish only after legally operative Terms fields and
   verified contact routes are supplied.
4. **LEGAL1-04 — Refund & Void status**: status-only page while self-service billing remains absent.
5. **LEGAL1-05 — Footer links**: add only links whose destinations exist; preserve authenticated
   shell invariants.

Slices may be combined when the resulting change remains independently reviewable and does not
cross the exclusions above.

## Verification

At minimum:

- route inventory tests prove only the six named public destinations exist under this capability;
- Arabic/English parity tests cover every page;
- structural tests prove no inline script/style or third-party tracker is introduced;
- content tests reject unresolved placeholder markers on publishable pages;
- content tests reject certification claims not explicitly allow-listed by verified repository
  evidence;
- a no-customer-context test proves legal pages do not resolve actor, organization, membership,
  dataset, analysis, or report state;
- footer-link tests prove every rendered legal link resolves to an implemented destination;
- the normal repository gates pass: `uv run khepri-gov validate`, `uv run ruff check .`, and
  `uv run pytest`.

## Implementation preconditions

1. `RCA-003` is `active` in `governance/registry.yaml`.
2. Each implementation slice states which `LEGAL1-*` unit it implements.
3. Any page with legally operative identity/contact/jurisdiction fields remains unpublished until
   those fields are verified.
4. Product code remains subject to active `RCA-001`/`RCA-002` boundaries where it touches shared
   runtime or shell code.
