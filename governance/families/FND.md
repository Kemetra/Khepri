# FND: Platform Foundation

Platform Foundation owns:

- repository governance and change control;
- stable artifact identifiers and the active/retired lifecycle;
- one machine-readable governance registry;
- fail-closed validation and its evidence;
- repository controls needed to enforce the governed workflow.

It excludes customer features, business or domain logic, infrastructure services, and
responsibilities of product families. Those boundaries require active families and specifications.

Identity, state, document, dependencies, and supersession are authoritative in
`governance/registry.yaml`.
